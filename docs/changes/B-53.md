## Why

Copilot does not surface the wrong patient file to the wrong employee. An open link or an unlabelled records site is what it would read from. The report finds them per client and shows how each point of the score was earned.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Ana, who owns Contoso Dental's Patient Records site, is shown that an Everyone-except-external link and a missing label put it at risk, before Copilot can quote a recall list to the front desk.
- L2: Contoso Dental scores 55 out of 100 with three named findings; Fabrikam Capital, the clean twin, scores 100. Each practice or firm sees exactly what to fix.
- L3: the MSP gets one comparable score per client with the formula printed, so the number can be checked and defended; label and DLP gaps are advice, not changes made on a client's behalf (C-20: one client's file at a time).
- L4: fewer small clinics and firms switching on Copilot with patient or client files open to everyone.

## Honest limits

- Every Graph, SharePoint and Purview field name is UNVERIFIED: written from memory, no Microsoft page opened. The fixture is flatter than the real shapes.
- Weights (15, 10, 20) are invented for a fixture-based pipeline check, not calibrated on real tenants.
- Organization-wide links, inheritance and group membership are not modelled. Site sensitivity is given in the fixture, not detected from content.
- No test was run by the builder; counts are for the next station to report.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx Copilot readiness is report-only and per client (B-53, 2026-10-22). One score out of 100 is `max(0, 100 - sum(points of every finding))`, with fixed weights per finding kind (Everyone or anyone link, guest share, unlabelled sensitive site); the formula, weights and counts are printed with each report. Label and DLP gaps are recommendations with 0 points and no action. No lever is added because nothing is written; label and policy changes stay with a person. Why: W-M365-07, W-PRJ-04, W-M365-08; C-25, C-21, C-20. Alternatives: a lever to apply labels or tighten links (rejected for this stage: tenant-wide policy reach). What would change it: the MSP's real tenant data and a calibrated weight set.
