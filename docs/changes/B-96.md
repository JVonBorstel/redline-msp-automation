## Why

Staff and clients get the same true account of what the AI does, and every sentence about the system is tied to a test or a file, so a page cannot say more than the repo shows.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: a technician learns to read a suggestion, a diff and an approval in ten minutes on three synthetic tickets, and what to do when the AI is wrong.
- L2: a client owner gets two one-page, plain-language statements of what the program does and where data goes, with the gaps named.
- L3: the MSP gets one set of statements that sales, support and leadership can all use, with a checker that stops them drifting from the tests.
- L4: small regulated businesses hear what an AI helper does and does not do, without a compliance claim nobody can back.
