## Why

Account managers spend their time on the conversation, not the slides. The QBR draft and the weekly digest start from numbers that were checked against their sources before the draft existed (SOC 2 evidence for C-21), and a person reads them before anything goes to a client.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: an account manager opens a QBR draft that already shows 3 failed backup jobs against 0 the quarter before, with a suggestion to review them, instead of building the slide by hand.
- L2: each document holds one client only; the isolation test searches every page and record for the other clients' names, ticket ids and backup organization names.
- L3: the MSP lead reviews drafts that reconcile with their sources; the digest lists closed tickets by number only, so ticket text (untrusted) never reaches a client-facing page.
- L4: smaller clients get the same weekly and quarterly view as larger ones, from the same fixtures and rules.

## Honest limits

- The QBR compares one real fixture month (October) with one invented month (September); it says how many months each quarter has and shows `n/a` rather than comparing unequal coverage. No full-quarter trend exists in the fixtures.
- Fixture shapes are this repository's own and UNVERIFIED against any vendor (U-04). Every figure is fixture-based. A win is a figure that moved, not a claim of value or savings.
- The digest's changes are baseline-run counts, not a change log; it does not say what each ticket was about.
- Draft only: no approve, send or schedule step exists; the Outlook draft is W9. No real model was called, no network, nothing pushed.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx The QBR and the weekly digest are reconciled after rendering and drafted only (B-72, 2026-10-27). The QBR is built from B-71's `build_report` for each month, compares quarters only when both have data (and, for counts, the same number of months), and lists wins, risks and suggestions by fixed rules; the digest lists closed tickets by id, channel and dates only, never ticket text. Each page is recomputed from the raw files by separate code and nothing is written on a mismatch. Both say DRAFT first and carry no reviewer. Why: W-RPT-02, W-RPT-03; C-21, C-45. Alternatives: letting a model write the narrative (rejected: a changed number or an injected instruction could reach a client), quoting ticket subjects in the digest (rejected: untrusted text), an hours or money figure (rejected: nothing in the fixtures supports it). What would change it: the MSP's real QBR format, a change-record source, and the W9 Outlook draft step.
