## Why

Hours of copy-paste per questionnaire become a review of cited drafts, without the draft saying more than anyone can show.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Ana gets a Contoso Dental security questionnaire back with each answer quoted from a named policy or client record, or marked `needs a person`, and the one owner who must review it named at the top.
- L2: A Fabrikam Capital fact or project never appears in a Contoso Dental draft; a client question the client's own records cannot answer is refused instead of filled from MSP policy.
- L3: The MSP sees a count of cited versus refused items per questionnaire and a standing rule: no certification, compliance, production-operation or result claim is ever drafted.
- L4: Fewer wrong answers on insurance applications and security reviews, because the draft cannot be stronger than its evidence and a person signs.

## Honest limits

See "Known limits" in the spec. In short: invented questionnaires, policy and hours; word-overlap retrieval; keyword refusal rules; tests cited are checked to exist, not run here; no export format; the owner is a name string.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx Answer drafts are quotes plus fixed leads, never generated prose (B-60, 2026-10-27). A claim is a fixed lead that names the kind of statement plus a verbatim quote, a control objective or an hours range; `check_draft` re-derives each claim from its citations, and strong words or numbers must come from the evidence. Certification, results and "enforced / in production" questions are refused outright because this repo can never evidence them. Why: W-CMP-09, W-CMP-08, W-PRJ-06; C-26, C-45, C-41. Alternative: a model paraphrase checked by a groundedness judge (rejected for now: a paraphrase is where strength creeps in, and the judge is another unverified model). What would change it: a measured, reviewed paraphrase that passes the same `check_draft` on real forms.
