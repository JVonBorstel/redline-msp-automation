## Why

Mail should not be spoofable, and nothing should expire by surprise on a Monday. A secret that lapses overnight breaks an integration; an expired GDAP relationship breaks support; a GDAP relationship with Global Administrator is more reach than the MSP should hold (D6-09). Each is a date or a policy that a script can read and a named person can act on.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Ana, the technician who owns Contoso Dental's backup connector, is told 12 days ahead that its secret ends on 2026-11-02, instead of finding out when backups fail overnight. A practice's front desk keeps working mail and VPN.
- L2: Contoso Dental's `smile.` domain has no SPF, DKIM or DMARC, so anyone can send as the practice; the report names it and its owner. Fabrikam Capital, the clean twin, shows nothing to fix.
- L3: the MSP sees every expiry and every Global Administrator relationship in one list, with an owner on each row; an item without an owner is shown as `unassigned` so it gets one. The module has no write path (C-38, C-20).
- L4: fewer spoofed practice emails reaching patients, and fewer outages at small clinics and firms that cannot absorb a surprise lapse.

## Honest limits

- Every vendor field (Partner Center GDAP, Graph application, the typed RFC 7489 element list) and the Global Administrator role id are UNVERIFIED: written from memory, no vendor page opened. The GDAP fixture shape is flatter than the real one.
- Fixtures are synthetic and written with the checks in view. DNS is read as record text; no live lookup, no nested-include counting, no key-strength or chain checks.
- EasyDMARC's own export and API are not imitated; only the RFC 7489 aggregate XML it collects.
- Owners are synthetic identity ids; a real owner map is a later adapter job.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx Expiry watch is report-only and per client (B-54, 2026-10-21). One list of reminder rows (kind, subject, status, due date, owner, `action: reminder-only`) covers SPF/DKIM/DMARC policy, DMARC aggregate results, app secrets and certificates, TLS certificates and GDAP relationships (expiring, expired, or holding Global Administrator). The window is N days (default 30; GDAP at least 60). Dates come from `as_of`, never the clock. Credentials are metadata only; a fixture field that could hold a secret is refused at load. Aggregate XML with a DTD or entity is refused. No lever is added because nothing is written; renewals, DNS changes and relationships stay human (W-VND-06, D6-09). Why: W-M365-10, W-IDM-12, W-VND-05, W-IDM-10; C-38, C-20, C-21. Alternatives: a renew lever (rejected: R3 identity reach and secrets must not pass through an agent); live DNS lookups (rejected for tests and this stage). What would change it: the MSP's real GDAP role templates, owner map and EasyDMARC export.
