## Why

Resolved tickets are where new KB articles come from, and KB articles rot or disagree. Drafting from the recorded fix saves the technician the write-up; the draft is labelled, redacted and left for a person to publish, so a requester's name, a patient's details, a secret or another client's text cannot ride into the knowledge base unreviewed.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Ana closes a Contoso Dental MFA ticket and finds a labelled draft with her recorded steps and the patient and caller details already replaced by placeholders, and a note that the print-spooler article says the opposite of the newer one.
- L2: Contoso's drafts and checks use Contoso's tickets and articles only; Fabrikam's text, and the MSP runbooks, never enter them.
- L3: the MSP gets a repeatable list of drafts to review and articles to fix, each with the ticket or article ids, instead of finding out when a technician follows the wrong page.
- L4: a KB that stays current and consistent without any client's data leaking across clients.

## Honest limits

- Fixture-based throughout: the tickets are B-21's, the resolutions and planted KB are invented, the retrieval is a word-overlap stand-in and the conflict rules are lexical. Real recall and precision are unknown; paraphrased contradictions are missed.
- Step coverage is close to 100% by construction (steps are copied); it checks that redaction does not drop steps.
- No real ticketing system, KB tool or model was used; how a real PSA stores a resolution is UNKNOWN.
- No lever, schedule, ticket or KB write exists; a person runs the commands and publishes by hand.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx KB drafts are files for a person to publish, never writes (B-58, 2026-10-27). Drafts are built from the recorded resolution only, redacted, per client, and carry `human_publish.required`; stale and conflict detection is read-only and compares only one client's own articles. Why: W-KB-01, W-KB-05, C-12, C-25, C-41; a draft that could publish itself would let ticket text reach a KB without review. Alternatives rejected: a publish lever behind approval (not asked for; the registry is outside this item's `owns`), comparing across clients (breaks C-25).
