## Why

AIR-11 (help-desk social engineering) is stopped at the runner, not only by the approving lead's attention. IR-2 gets a real kill switch, and the never-log list (STD-AU-4 item 6) is enforced for the one lever that touches contact details.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Priya Nair (synthetic, ticket T-3101) cannot have her MFA reset because a caller sounded convincing. The technician must call her back on the number on file and record that, and her phone number never lands in the audit log.
- L2: a dental practice or an RIA can ask "who verified the caller?" and get the method, the person and the time from one record. A lead can stop resets for that one client in seconds if something looks wrong.
- L3: the MSP gets C-30 and C-31 as working controls with audit evidence. The audit log holds less personal data, so there is less to protect and less to disclose.
- L4: the reset step attackers talk their way through now needs out-of-band proof at every client at once, and any lead can pull the switch during an incident without waiting for approval.

## Honest limits

- The verification record is an attestation: the runner checks that it is present, scoped and not future-dated, not that the call really happened. Its age is not limited.
- A pause file is per state folder and per process lock. Cross-process writers are out of scope, the same as the audit log (Phase 1).
- Any lead may pause or resume a lever for all clients, including clients outside their own list. Global resume is the risky direction; restricting it is an open question for RQ/governance.
- Masking is by known keys plus a `+`-prefixed phone pattern. A national-format number under an unknown key would pass (redaction is a backstop, STD-AU-5).
- `pause` and `resume` reuse the v1 audit `mode` field; STD-AU 4.1 lists `dry-run`, `execute`, `approve` and `reject` and needs a docs line.
- `docs/DEMO.md`, `docs/COVERAGE.md` and governance 6.1/AIR-11/EU-4 status rows are not in this item's `owns`; they need the docs follow-up listed below.

## DECISIONS note proposal

- Pauses live in `pauses.json` beside the audit log, so every runner on one state folder shares them without new wiring. Only a lead may pause or resume. Pausing needs no approval (IR-2). A global pause wins over a client pause. An unreadable pause file denies every call (fail closed).
- `verification: "requester"` levers need `Approval.verification` to execute. It must be by a known identity scoped to the client and not future-dated, and it is written to `result.verification`.
- The audit copy of a diff keeps only type and id for authentication methods and masks contact keys and `+`-phone strings. The caller keeps the full diff.
