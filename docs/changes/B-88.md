## Why

Approvals and drafts should arrive where technicians already work, without opening a second way to approve. The card shows the same diff, undo and impact the page shows and links back to the page; deciding stays with a signed-in lead there.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Ana sees in Teams that a change for Contoso Dental waits, what it does to the person, and how to undo it; the person is protected because the decision still needs a lead's sign-in and the requester check.
- L2: Contoso Dental's digest is a draft in a technician's mailbox addressed only to the client's own domains; its documents land only in `client:contoso-dental`.
- L3: the MSP gets prompts and drafts without a new approval path, and every refused card response is in the client's audit chain.
- L4: fewer stale approvals and late digests, with no client's text or addresses crossing into another's.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx Card responses never decide (B-88, 2026-10-27). The Teams card is a notification with one `Action.OpenUrl` to the loopback approval page; a card response is audited as denied by `teams-card` and redirected. Why: a card click cannot carry a verified Entra sign-in, scope check, four-eyes or requester verification, so any decision power on the card would bypass B-03 and B-89. Alternative: validate the card response like a UI decision and decide (rejected until a verified sign-in exists on that path). What would change it: a verified Teams identity bound to an Entra token that the approval flow already accepts.
