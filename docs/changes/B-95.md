## Why

Leadership can rank the next automation by measured value and risk; an assessor or client can see which controls are claimed, what backs each, and what is not shown. Both pages are generated from the code, the map and the eval reports, so they cannot drift.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: a technician with an idea gets a same-day answer with the rule behind it, instead of a hallway opinion.
- L2: a client is not offered an automation that touches secrets, or sends regulated data to a hosted model, because the intake blocks it before design.
- L3: the MSP gets one ranked list, a control-to-criterion table whose every claim names a test, and a register with owner roles.
- L4: unsafe ideas (help-desk identity checks, password retrieval) stay with people at every client the MSP serves.
