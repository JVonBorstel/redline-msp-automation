# B-04 change note: affected-person notice drafts

Status: **built** (drafts, CLI, tests). Sending stays human (HO-9). Synthetic data only.

## What changed

- `workflows/notify/` (new package): `notify.py` loads one proposal from `<state>/queue.jsonl` (read only), refuses anything not `executed` (`NotifyRefused`), fills `templates/<lever>.md` from trusted fields with `string.Template.substitute` (a missing value fails closed), prefixes `Draft. A person reviews and sends this.` and writes `<state>/notices/<proposal-id>.md` atomically. `__main__.py` is the CLI (exit 0/1/2 per STD-ER-6). `README.md` per STD-DC-7.
- `workflows/notify/templates/user.reset-mfa.md`, `user.disable.md`: one template per write lever (DOD-5).
- `fixtures/notify/contacts.json`: synthetic fast-path line per client (`555-0190` contoso-dental, `555-0191` fabrikam-capital; 555-01xx fictional range, chosen not to collide with any tenant phone), team name and hours. Schema-checked at load, fails closed.
- `tests/py/notify/test_b04_notify.py`: 21 tests named `test_b04_<k>_...` for B04-1 to B04-4, including isolation (contoso draft never shows the fabrikam line) and a seeded tenant with a personal phone and a secret-shaped note that never reach the draft.
- `specs/B-04.md`: acceptance ids, refined from the roadmap.

## Why

EU-1 says the person hears, in plain words, what was done to their account and how to get back; EU-2 says there is one fast way back. Until now the change was audited and approved, but the person it happened to heard nothing unless a technician wrote it from scratch.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Priya Nair (synthetic, T-3101) gets a 101-word note: her MFA was reset, her password is the same, Lee approved it, how to set it up again, and a number to call that puts her first.
- L2: Contoso Dental staff get the same message shape for every automated change, so the practice is never surprised by "the system did it".
- L3: the MSP sends consistent, reviewed notices without writing each one; a write lever cannot ship without a template (test fails), and the draft never contains free text, secrets or personal numbers.
- L4: people affected by automation are told what happened and how to undo it, which is the trust condition for automating identity work at all (IM-11, chains W-IDM-02, W-ONB-08).

## DECISIONS note proposal

D-0xx Notice drafts are template-filled, never model-written (B-04, 2026-10-21). Each write lever has `workflows/notify/templates/<lever>.md`; values come only from the proposal, ticket form fields, tenant `displayName`, the identity store and `fixtures/notify/contacts.json`. Only `executed` proposals get a draft; the first line is the HO-9 header. Why: a notice to an affected person is client-facing text about a security change; deterministic templates can be reviewed once and tested for length, grade, secrets and phone numbers. Alternative: a model drafts from the diff (more natural, but untestable for leakage and needs a review step per message). What would change it: per-client wording or languages that templates cannot cover.
