## Why

Compute that cannot leak a key because none exists, and a pipeline where a person must read the what-if and approve before anything could deploy, with the deploy stage off.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Ana, a technician, can see what a change will do to Azure before anyone approves it.
- L2: a client's automation runs under one identity with no stored login to leave behind.
- L3: the MSP gets a repeatable lint, what-if and approval path, and a written rollout and rollback rule.
- L4: fewer long-lived credentials and fewer unreviewed infrastructure changes at small practices and firms.
