# Change: B-37 fact packs for human-only identity decisions

## What changed

- `workflows/factpack/` (new): `python -m workflows.factpack <termination|caller|role|policy> --client <id> --target <id>` prints a read-only fact pack for one person or one Conditional Access policy of one client.
- `workflows/factpack/fixtures/devices.json` (new): a synthetic device list; no device source existed. Every field name is UNVERIFIED.
- `docs/runbooks/urgent-termination.md`, `docs/runbooks/caller-verification.md` (new): the human procedures. `docs/runbooks/` did not exist before.
- `specs/B-37.md`, `tests/py/factpack/test_b37_factpack.py` (new). No existing file was edited.

## Why

Termination, caller verification, Conditional Access changes and privileged-role grants are R3 and "none-keep-human" (W-ONB-15, W-IDM-04, W-IDM-06, W-IDM-07). The tool's only job is to put the facts in front of the human who decides, faster, with no lever, proposal or model in the loop.

## Effect by level

- **L1 person**: the person who is let go, or who calls in, is handled with the facts checked and the callback done, so fewer wrong lockouts and fewer impostors let in. Contact details are never shown.
- **L2 client business**: a termination happens at the minute HR and the client contact agreed, with sessions and devices known in advance; a Conditional Access change is judged with its exclusions and scope in view.
- **L3 MSP**: a lead and a technician decide the hardest cases in minutes, not after hunting across portals; the approval record keeps the callback method.
- **L4 community**: less help-desk impersonation risk (the attack CISA AA23-320A describes) reaching client staff and their customers.

## Caveats

- The tenant fixtures and the Graph baseline fixtures describe partly different people, so a person's pack may show a source as a gap. This is stated in `gaps`, not filled.
- `config/clients/` is not committed in this branch, so the adapter is built with a local read-only binding rather than `build_graph_adapter`.
- Session counts are the mock's INVENTED counter.

## DECISIONS proposal

**D-B37-1 Fact packs are assembly only.** A fact pack module may read adapters and fixtures but never imports or calls a lever runner, queue or approval code, and its output always carries `leversRegistered: []` and `proposals: []`. Any step that acts on a fact pack is a separate lever with its own approvals (R3: two distinct human leads). Basis: the NEVER AUTOMATE rules of W-ONB-15, W-IDM-04, W-IDM-06, W-IDM-07; D6-05, D6-06, D6-07. Revisit when the MSP defines its own termination and verification procedure.
