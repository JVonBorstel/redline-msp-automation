## Why

- Prices, hours, effort and risk labels and thresholds are invented stand-ins (UNVERIFIED). Budgets are ranges, not quotes.
- Industry per client is declared in `data/clients.json` (no client record file is committed); hardware class is guessed from the hostname.
- Litware Legal has no licensing, device or backup coverage data: its roadmap has health items only and says so.
- The policy draft is not legal advice and claims no compliance; the regimes shown come from a fixture and are marked UNVERIFIED. A hosted entry in the vendor register does not switch a provider on.
- No model is called. Nothing schedules the run.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx Roadmap items are rule-derived and citation-bound (B-73, 2026-10-27). A recommendation exists only if a rule fires on a finding from that client's own reports, and it carries the report, field, value and SHA-256 of each source file; `Recommendation` refuses an empty citation and `verify_draft` rebuilds the record from the sources. The AI-use policy draft is generated from `governance/AI_POLICY.md` and the vendor register at build time, and hosted PHI or NPI is never offered whatever a register entry lists. Why: W-RPT-04, W-RPT-05; C-21, C-42, C-45. Alternative: model-written recommendations (rejected: not checkable). What would change it: the MSP's real price book and a committed client record file.
