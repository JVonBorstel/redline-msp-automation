## Why

Clients are billed for what their contract says, and notes explain the work. A lead gets a first pass that names the contract clause behind each suggestion and says what is missing from each note, instead of re-deriving both from scratch.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Ana, a technician, sees which of her notes lack a reason or an outcome, with a draft to edit, without a score attached to her name.
- L2: Contoso Dental's report cites the clause behind each suggestion, so a disputed line can be checked against the contract rather than argued.
- L3: the lead sees contract-covered, project, billable and non-billable hours and the entries a person must classify; every run is in the client's audit chain.
- L4: fewer surprise bills and billing disputes at small practices and firms. Not measured here.

## Honest limits

- UNVERIFIED against any real system: the contract clauses, caps and time-entry fields are invented; no real MSA, PSA billing-code list or Autotask time-entry entity was read. Fixture-based only.
- 23/23 on the gold labels shows consistency with one author's reading, not accuracy; fixtures, rules and gold share an author, and one vague-word adjustment was made while hand-checking. No model is called.
- The note rubric is English keyword matching. It cannot tell a true note from a plausible one and does not read the ticket.
- Caps are monthly, per clause, by entry date; increments, rollover, blocks and tiered rates are not modelled. First matching clause wins.
- Nothing records an approval or applies a proposal; no PSA write, no invoice feed, no schedule. A malformed client id or date is refused before the audit step and is not audited.
- No technician appears in any output; that is by design (NN-03), so there is also no way to see which technician needs help with notes.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx Billing classification is rule-based, clause-cited and proposal-only (B-76, 2026-10-27). Structured fields decide the category; free-text notes never do and an instruction in one is flagged. Note quality is a fixed, recomputable score with cited reasons. Corrections are proposals (`proposed`, no approval, not applied); `verify` refuses a file claiming otherwise. No technician-level output exists. Why: W-BIL-01, W-BIL-03; C-14, C-41; NN-02, NN-03. Alternative: a model to classify and score notes (rejected for now: no eval shows a rule gap that needs one, and rules are explainable by clause). What would change it: a real contract and real notes showing the rules miss often.
