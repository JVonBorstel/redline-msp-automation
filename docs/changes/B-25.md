# B-25 change note: draft replies with KB citations and closure follow-ups

Status: **built** (workflow, CLI, eval, tests). Sending stays human (HO-9). Synthetic data only.

## What changed

- `workflows/replies/` (new): `replies.py` (`draft_reply`, `draft_closure`, `check_citations`, a ticket loader that ignores labels), `__main__.py` (CLI, exit 0/1/2), `README.md`.
- `evals/replies/` (new): `gold.py` (Phase 2 tickets with their answer key, evals only), `report.py` (writes `report.md`), `report.md` (generated, fixture-based).
- `tests/py/replies/test_b25_replies.py`: tests named `test_b25_<k>_...` for B25-1 to B25-3.
- `specs/B-25.md`: acceptance ids refined from the roadmap.

No lever, registry, identity, fixture or other package was changed. No model is called, so there is no new prompt; the citation validator is what a later model-written draft must pass.

## Why

A first reply is the slowest part of a ticket for the person waiting, and the failure to avoid is an assistant that promises something untrue (AIR-4). Quoting only the client's own KB, with an id on every sentence and a hard `no draft: no source article`, makes the draft checkable by the technician in seconds and by a test in milliseconds.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Hannah Ortiz (synthetic, Litware Legal, T-5301, new phone with a filing due at 5:00 pm) gets a cited first answer pointing to the firm's own MFA guide instead of waiting for a technician to write one; when the ticket is resolved she gets a plain "it is fixed, check it, here is the number if it is not".
- L2: a client's practice or firm is never told something its own documentation does not say, and nothing from another client's documents can appear in its replies.
- L3: the MSP drafts in seconds from existing documentation, finds weak articles by what gets `no source`, and every search is in the audit chain. A technician still reads and sends.
- L4: the Air Canada pattern (an assistant inventing a policy) is closed in a way a test proves, which is the trust condition for assistants answering people at small businesses at all.

## Eval result (fixture-based, 210 synthetic Phase 2 tickets)

Citation coverage 100% (192 of 192 sentences), cross-client citations 0. 62 tickets drafted (Contoso 8, Fabrikam 6, Litware 48), 133 `no source`, 15 injection tickets refused. Source recall on the 49 labelled tickets 34 (69%); 6 get a cited draft from the wrong article; 21 of 161 "no source" tickets get a draft from a loosely related article. 85 of 136 resolved tickets got a closure draft (Litware has no contact). The `MIN_SCORE` threshold was tuned on these same fixtures.

## Honest limits

- `kb.search` is enabled only for contoso-dental and fabrikam-capital in `levers/registry.json`, and `triage-bot` is scoped to those two. The CLI therefore refuses Litware tickets with `no draft: the knowledge base search was not allowed (...)`. The eval runs on a throwaway registry copy and an eval-only agent that add Litware. **Note for the owner of the registry and identities (B-18 or the integrator):** add `litware-legal` to the `kb.search` `client_scope` and a Litware agent scope when Litware identities land.
- `fixtures/notify/contacts.json` has no Litware entry, so Litware closure drafts fail closed. The Contoso and Fabrikam KBs are thin (B-21 left extending them to this item; not done here because it would change Phase 1 `kb.search` results and those fixtures are not owned by B-25).
- Quoted KB sentences are technician-voiced; a person edits them. The draft is grounded but may not answer the ticket (see the wrong-article numbers).
- There is no record that a person sent a draft, and the drafting itself is not an audit event (only the `kb.search` call is).
- The workflow loader reads `fixtures/tickets/**` and ignores labels (STD-FX-5); `evals/replies/gold.py` is the only place that names the Phase 2 folder.

## DECISIONS note proposal

D-0xx Reply drafts are quoted from the client's KB, not written by a model (B-25, 2026-10-21). A reply draft consists only of sentences of articles returned by `kb.search` for the ticket's own client, each followed by `[<article id>]`; with no source the output is `no draft: no source article`. Ticket text is used only as search keywords, after the injection scan. Closure follow-ups are templates filled from trusted fields. Both start with the draft label and are never sent by code. `check_citations` is the gate for any future model-written draft. Why: the Air Canada pattern (AIR-4); a draft a technician can verify line by line. Alternative: a model paraphrases the article (friendlier, but needs the same validator plus a faithfulness eval). What would change it: a measured model paraphrase that keeps 100% citation coverage and passes a faithfulness check on the gold set.
