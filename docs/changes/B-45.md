## Why

The user who reports a phish gets an answer, and the next user is protected: the draft answers the reporter, `message.trace` finds who else got it, and a wrongly held message can be released with a lead's approval, never one held as high-confidence phish or malware.

## Individual to macro

- **L1 Sam Patel (one person at Contoso Dental):** reports a fake password notice and gets a draft answer naming the failed DMARC, the lookalike domain and the IP-address link, without anyone clicking it.
- **L2 Contoso Dental:** the two colleagues who got the same message are found by one trace; a catalogue wrongly held as bulk is released to Priya after a lead approves.
- **L3 the MSP:** Defender and INKY reports take one path, every release is approved and audited, and an email that tells the AI to release itself gets no lever.
- **L4 The community:** a hostile message cannot steer the tooling into releasing phish; another client's message id answers like an unknown one.
