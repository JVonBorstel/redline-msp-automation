## Why

A wrong automated action at a dental or financial client can also be a breach. The person should be back at work first, the client should hear from a human the same day, and the privacy officer should get the facts early enough for their own deadlines.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Priya, a hygienist, is signed out by mistake and gets an apology, a fix and the fast phone line, from a person.
- L2: Contoso Dental's practice manager hears about it first from the MSP, with what was fixed and paused, and gets a review date.
- L3: the MSP gets one rehearsed procedure with commands that exist, a one-client evidence export and a lessons list (global pause side effects, an untrue draft reason, no audit query command).
- L4: fewer small practices and firms left waiting while an MSP finds out who to call, and a privacy officer who can meet their own deadlines.
