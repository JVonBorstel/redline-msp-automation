## Why

B-56 made the index safe to read. B-57 is the read side: a technician gets passages and answers they can check, and a measured proof that another client's canaries, poisoned documents and vault strings do not come out.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Ana asks why the front-office printer is offline and gets Contoso Dental's own sentences, each with the chunk id to open; if there is no article, she gets `no source found`, not a guess.
- L2: Contoso Dental's questions are answered from `client:contoso-dental` plus MSP runbooks. Fabrikam Capital's VPN article is never searched, and the filter is in the store.
- L3: the MSP gets one lever with no client parameter, an eval that asks every question as every caller, and a threshold chosen by an eval instead of by opinion.
- L4: fewer cases where one practice's or firm's notes reach another, and fewer confident wrong answers.

## Results (fixture-based; see `evals/rag/report.md`)

- Leaks: 0 over 150 question-by-caller runs at the chosen threshold (0.38) and 0 at threshold 0.0; 0 chunks outside the caller's namespaces; 0 store errors.
- Groundedness 100% (56 answered sentences, all cited and quoted). By construction, not a quality measure.
- Dev: all should-refuse questions refused; 90% of answerable answered with the expected article. Holdout: 86.7% of should-refuse refused (2 of 15 answered, both generic-wording canary probes answered from the caller's own articles), 70% of answerable answered.

## Honest limits

- `HashEmbedder` is lexical. The threshold and every relevance number describe it and 50 hand-written questions, not a real embedder or real tickets (UNVERIFIED with any real model). A real embedder needs the eval re-run and the threshold re-chosen.
- The injected and vault documents in the eval are caught by design, so a zero shows the pipeline holds when the tripwire works. A missed injection would be indexed and could be quoted.
- Leaks are detected by exact planted strings; structural namespace checks cover the rest.
- The answers are quotes. The store is in memory. `evals/gates.json` has no B-57 entry (B-84 owns it).

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx Retrieval answers are quoted, cited and refusable (B-57, 2026-10-27). Answers consist of verbatim sentences from retrieved passages, each ending with chunk ids; below the threshold the answer is `no source found`. The caller's namespaces are `client:<caller>` and `msp-internal`, chosen in code from the runner's client (no client parameter) and applied in the store. The threshold is set by the eval on a dev split and pinned to the code by a test. Why: W-KB-04; C-26, C-27, C-41. Alternative: model-written answers with a judge (rejected for now: nothing here may call a model, and a quote cannot hallucinate). What would change it: a real embedder and model adapter, with this eval re-run first.
