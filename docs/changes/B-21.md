## Why

Phase 2 needs a gold set big enough to measure summaries, duplicate grouping, SLA risk, cited replies and redaction, and a third client so isolation tests compare three tenants. The vertical mix follows the MSP's published market (financial services, RIAs, legal; D6-13).

- L1: Hannah Ortiz (synthetic paralegal, Litware Legal) has a 140-page filing stuck on the copier at 3:40 pm with a 5:00 pm deadline (T-5341); the set labels it P1 with the copier article as the reply source, so later evals check she gets a correct, cited answer in minutes.

## Honest limits

- One author wrote the tickets and the labels; wording is tidier than real tickets, so later scores are pipeline checks, not model quality (D-022).
- Contoso and Fabrikam keep their thin KBs (3 and 2 articles; not owned here), so only 11 of their 130 tickets have a reply source. Extending those KBs would change `kb.search` results that the Phase 1 tests rely on, so it is left to `B-25`.
- `tech-cara` (Litware technician) is not in `config/identities.json`; no identity can act on `litware-legal` yet. Adding Litware identities belongs to `B-18` (D6-14).
- SLA tiers are fixed here; the response and resolution targets per tier are `B-24`'s matrix (UNVERIFIED against the MSP's real SLAs).
- The rule-based injection detector flagged 15 of 15 labelled attacks and 0 of 195 clean tickets when the set was written; the tickets were written with its rules in view, so this is a consistency check only.
