## Why

A leaver's mailbox and OneDrive are handled by hand many times a month ("manager gets full mailbox access without a business reason", "access granted too broadly"). Each step becomes one named change: a diff the approver reads, an undo for the pairs that have one, an end date on the OneDrive access, and a notice to the person it affects. Forwarding to an outside address is left out on purpose: it is the classic mail-compromise move, and `W-M365-05` flags it.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Jamie Chen (synthetic) left Contoso Dental. Ana Lopez, the office manager, gets Full Access and a OneDrive grant that ends on a date the approver signed (at most 30 days); callers hear who to contact from an auto-reply; the mailbox becomes shared so its mail is kept. The cost of a wrong grant is that the wrong person reads private or regulated mail, so the delegate is named in the approval and only a same-client user can be named.
- L2: the client keeps a leaver's mail and files without paying for the licence, and access to them has an end date. A mailbox over 50 GB (Alex Ward at Fabrikam Capital, synthetic) is stopped for a licence decision rather than failing later.
- L3: five inventory items (`W-M365-01`, `-02`, `-09`, `W-ONB-09`, `-10`) move from unrecorded hand edits to registered, approved, audited changes; counts and diffs reconcile; the same request twice is one change.
- L4: another client's person or list answers exactly like an absent one, a write to one client's file cannot touch another's, and no lever can send a mailbox's mail to an outside address.

## Honest limits

- Nothing was checked against Exchange Online or SharePoint documentation or a tenant. The module is a mock over a JSON file; it proves the control shape (diff, approval, undo, guards), not the vendor calls.
- Time-bound means the end date is part of what the approver signs and the module refuses long or past dates. **Nothing ends the grant on its date**; a person or a scheduled job has to run `mailbox.revoke-onedrive-manager`. That job is not built here.
- The 50 GB limit and the `SiteCollectionAdmin` access level are recalled from memory (UNVERIFIED). A narrower OneDrive right may exist; the broad one is the realistic mechanism and is the least-privilege question for the MSP.
- A refusal inside the module (wrong client, outside address, date out of range, oversized mailbox) reaches the Python caller as `<lever> crashed: RuntimeException`, because the host reports a thrown error by type only. The sentence is only visible when the function is called directly. A later change could add an expected-failure channel to the host.
- The lever cannot tell whether the delegate is the person the client named; that stays with the technician and the approver (`W-ONB-09`: "Delegate named by the client contact").
- The risk class follows the inventory (R2). Reviewers who treat granting access to a leaver's mail as an identity change (D6-07) can set `min_approvers: 2` in `levers/registry.d/mailbox.json`, or move the grant levers to R3, without code changes.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx Mailbox and OneDrive changes for joiners and leavers are R2 PowerShell levers with a fixed set of guards, and external forwarding is not offered (B-33, 2026-10-22). Twelve `mailbox.*` levers (list membership, Full Access, Send As, convert to shared, auto-reply, archive, a manager's OneDrive access) are R2 with lead approval, agent dry-run and technician execute. A delegate or manager must be a user of the same client; the OneDrive grant carries an end date in the approved params, after the as-of date and at most 30 days away; mailboxes over 50 GB are not converted; auto-reply text is pattern-checked. Forwarding to an outside address, inbox rules and holds are never a lever. Why: W-M365-01, W-M365-02, W-M365-09, W-ONB-09, W-ONB-10, C-05, C-06, D6-05, D6-07. Alternatives: R3 for access grants (rejected for now: the inventory says R2 and a grant is not an identity change; a per-lever `min_approvers` raises it), granting the OneDrive for as long as the technician likes (rejected: that is the pain the inventory names), an automatic expiry inside the lever (rejected: a scheduled job with its own audit is the right place, not built here). What would change it: the MSP's real tooling for mailbox work (UNKNOWN) and an auditor's view on access grants.
