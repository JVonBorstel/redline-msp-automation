## Why

Small regulated clients owe their own regulators evidence. A dental client maps to the HIPAA Security Rule, a financial client to the FTC Safeguards Rule, an adviser also to Regulation S-P. The pack lines up the repo's synthetic findings and controls against those items for the client's compliance owner, and tracks the vendors and BAAs that touch the client's data.

## Individual to macro (L1-L4)

- L1: Ana, a technician, hands Contoso Dental's office manager one pack: each HIPAA item, the governance row it comes from, the findings against it, and which vendors have no signed BAA.
- L2: a pack names only its own client; a lead must be scoped to that client; Litware Legal, with no regimes set, is refused rather than guessed.
- L3: the MSP builds the same pack for every client from data, with the regime list owned by the compliance owner; every build, dry run or refusal is in the client's audit chain and the manifest hash can be pinned there.
- L4: renewals and missing BAAs surface before an exam, and the mapping is re-checkable with `sha256sum -c`.

## Honest limits

- A mapping for the client's compliance owner; not legal advice, not a certification, no claim that any item is met. An item without a finding says "no finding supplied".
- Citations come from the governance map, written from memory, and are not checked against the eCFR or Federal Register. Regulation S-P is hedged on every line and cites the nearest `GLBA-n` row because the map has no S-P rows.
- Findings, vendors, contracts, BAA statuses and regimes are synthetic; topic matching is a convenience, not an analysis.
- The 365-day review interval is a fixture setting. AI-provider vendors belong to the C-43 register (B-90).
- Nothing schedules the run, retains output, or chases a renewal.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx Regulatory packs are mapping files, regimes come from data (B-52, 2026-10-27). A pack is Markdown pages plus JSON under a hashed manifest, built per client by a scoped lead, dry run by default, audited, and pinned in the client's chain. Which regimes apply is read from the client record that the compliance owner sets (D6-12); code never infers it. Reg S-P lines are hedged and cite the nearest GLBA row. A build is refused if a mapping cites a governance or control id that does not exist. Why: W-CMP-02, W-CMP-03, W-CMP-06; C-25, C-43, C-45. Alternative: a free-text compliance report (rejected: it would drift into legal claims). What would change it: counsel reading the rule text, or the MSP's real client regime list.
