# B-38 change note: guests, stale accounts and baseline-sourced proposals

Status: **built** against the **mock** tenant. Synthetic data only. Spec: `specs/B-38.md`. `tests/py/baseline` (25) passes locally on 2026-10-22; the full suites run in CI.

## What changed

- New workflow `workflows/baseline_proposals/` (`plan`, CLI `python -m workflows.baseline_proposals`, `config.json`, README). It reads a TenantBaseline `findings.json` and the client's guest fixture and proposes `user.disable` for stale members and expired guests. Each proposal is dry-run first, has `source: baseline` and the finding id as `ref`, and offers the `finding-reference` verification the lead records at approval. It never executes anything.
- Break-glass accounts are never proposed; the rule (name prefix and display-name pattern) is read from `TenantBaseline.config.json`, not copied.
- A cap, `max_proposals_per_client_per_run` in `config.json` (default 5), limits proposals per client per run; the rest are listed as `deferred`. Already-queued proposals and refused dry-runs do not use the cap.
- New fixture `fixtures/tenant/contoso-dental/guests.json` (synthetic: one expired, one valid, one disabled guest).
- New tests `tests/py/baseline/test_b38_baseline_proposals.py` (25, named `test_b38_<k>_...`).
- Nothing outside `owns` was changed.

## Why

Dormant accounts stay open doors, and a bulk disable nobody reviewed is its own risk. This turns the baseline's stale-account findings into single, reviewed, dry-run-first proposals that a lead approves one by one, with a hard limit on how many a run can queue.

## Individual to macro (L1 to L4)

- **L1, one person.** A person is only disabled after a lead looked at that one account; a break-glass or still-valid account is never in the list. The announcement to the person is the notify workflow (B-04), not this item.
- **L2, the client business.** Old accounts and expired guest access close without a big-bang cleanup; the cap keeps a bad day from locking out many people at once.
- **L3, the MSP.** Every proposal has an audited dry-run, four-eyes approval and a finding reference (C-30), so the audit shows why each account was disabled. Deferred items are listed, not dropped.
- **L4, community.** A small, config-driven pattern (finding, cap, break-glass rule from existing config) other checks can reuse.

## How the tests prove it

B38-1: stale finding becomes a pending `baseline` proposal with `ref` = finding id, an audited dry-run before it, and `finding-reference` verification; an expired guest is proposed (finding id when there is one, guest id otherwise); unexpired, disabled and bad-date guests are not; a lead's approval with that verification disables the account; the requester cannot approve; the real `Invoke-TenantBaseline` output feeds the workflow (skips without `pwsh`). B38-2: prefix, display-name and guest break-glass accounts are skipped, including no dry-run, and changing the baseline config changes the result. B38-3: cap 2 of 6 gives 2 proposals and 4 deferred with refs; the cap is per client; bad cap values (0, -1, true, 2.5, "3", 1001) are refused; a second run is idempotent and moves to the next batch. Supporting: empty, missing, malformed, wrong-tenant and schema-invalid inputs, client isolation and CLI exit codes.

## Not done, and caveats

- **Guest UPNs with `#EXT#` cannot be queued today.** `user.disable`'s registry `upn` pattern has no `#`, so the dry-run is refused and the guest is reported under `skipped` ("not queued"). The committed guest fixture uses that real shape on purpose, and a test pins the behaviour. The fix is a registry pattern change, outside this item's files.
- On the committed mock tenant the baseline's stale users are not present, so their dry-run is refused and reported; tests use a copy that has them.
- A pending proposal cannot hold a verification (store rule), so `verification` is offered as `Proposed.verification(by, at)` and shown in the JSON; the lead's approval step must pass it to `queue.decide`. The approval page (B-03) already offers `finding-reference`.
- `expiresOn` (guests) is this repo's own field, UNVERIFIED against Graph. The cap default 5 is not tuned on a real tenant.
- Not in scope: the person announcement (B-04) and bulk apply.

## DECISIONS proposal

D-0xx Baseline stale-account proposals are one proposal per account with a per-client cap (B-38, W3, 2026-10-22). The workflow proposes `user.disable` per stale member or expired guest, skips break-glass accounts by the baseline's own rule, and queues at most `max_proposals_per_client_per_run` (default 5) per client per run, listing the rest as deferred. Why: dormant accounts should close without a bulk change nobody reviewed. Alternative: one bulk proposal for all stale accounts, or no cap. What would change it: an incident where a cap of 5 delayed closing a real compromise, or an owner call on a different number.
