# Client conversation guide

Status: outline (written in Phase 10). For account managers talking to small, regulated clients about AI.

1. Plain-language summary: what we use AI for, what stays human.
2. Data handling: whose data goes where, what never leaves, isolation between clients.
3. Honest limits: what the system cannot promise; no real-world results claimed beyond what is measured and labeled.
4. Common questions (training on my data? who can see it? what if it is wrong? how do I opt out?) with careful, verified answers only.
5. What we will and will not commit to in writing.
