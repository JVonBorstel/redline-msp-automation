# Staff training: working with AI safely

Status: outline (written in Phase 10, for technicians and account managers, no AI background assumed).

1. What this is and is not: suggestions with reasons, never silent actions.
2. What may and may not go into a prompt (links to governance/DLP_MAPPING.md).
3. How to read a suggestion: why, which data, which lever it would pull.
4. Accept, correct, or reject; corrections improve the system.
5. Approvals: what you are signing off on and where to see the diff.
6. When the AI is wrong or odd: how to report it (AI incident response).
7. Ten-minute exercise using synthetic tickets.
