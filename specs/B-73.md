# B-73 Technology roadmap recommendations and the client AI-use policy draft

Status: **built** (workflow, CLI, template, tests). Drafts only: nothing is sent, posted or queued, and nothing in a tenant, device, licence or backup is changed. Synthetic data only.

> Independently built prototype. Not affiliated with any vendor's or real company's systems. Synthetic data only. Not legal advice.

Inventory: `W-RPT-04` (technology roadmap recommendations), `W-RPT-05` (client AI-use policy starter). Controls: `C-21` (every number checked against its source), `C-42`, `C-45`. Depends on `B-71` (health report). It also reads the finished `B-75` licensing report, `B-64` device reports and `B-70` backup coverage report. Standard: `STD-DC`.

## Why

Small clients get a credible plan and a first AI policy. The plan is derived from that client's own findings, so a person can check every line; the policy is the MSP's own AI policy filled with that client's parameters.

## Behaviour

`python -m workflows.reporting.roadmap` (package `workflows/reporting/roadmap/`):

- **`build <client> --period YYYY-MM --generated-on YYYY-MM-DD [--out-dir D]`** (or `--all`) writes under `<out-dir>/<period>/`: `<client>.roadmap.draft.md`, `<client>.roadmap.draft.json` and `<client>.ai-policy.draft.md`. Default folder `build/client-roadmap`. The run builds everything first; a refused run writes nothing. Dates come from the arguments, never the clock.
- **`verify <record.json>`** rebuilds the roadmap from the sources and compares it byte for byte with the record and its Markdown; it also re-hashes every cited file. Exit 0 matches, 1 mismatch, 2 bad input.

### Roadmap (`build_roadmap`)

One list per client, from that client's own sources only:

| Category | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| hardware | licensing report (`B-75`) `hardware[]` | lifecycle `past` or `ending-soon` (within 90 days) gives a replace or plan item; workstation or server price by hostname marker |
| licence | licensing report `seats[]`, `renewals[]` | reducible seats; subscription or domain renewals that are due or lapsed |
| security | health report (`B-71`) rules that fired | baseline high, Conditional Access change, critical or stale patching, phishing clicks, vault score, MFA and enrolment |
| backup | health backup rules; backup coverage report (`B-70`) | failed, missed or boot-check backup jobs; uncovered objects; policy issues; stale DR plan sections |
| devices | device reports (`B-64`) | non-compliant or stale devices, devices not in Intune, unencrypted disks, LAPS backup gaps |

Every item carries: an id, the rule, a title and action, an **effort** label and a **risk** label (`low`, `medium`, `high`; effort is the work to do it, risk is what leaving the finding alone could cost; both are stand-ins a person adjusts), a **budget range** with its basis, and its **evidence**: each finding as report, field, value and the source files with SHA-256 (CRLF read as LF). Items sort by category, then id. Totals add the one-time and per-year ranges.

Budget figures come from `workflows/reporting/roadmap/data/price_list.json` (an invented stand-in price list: hardware by class, labour hours at an hourly range per item) and from the client's licensing fixture (unit price per seat). A person replaces the price list with the MSP's real price book.

A source the client has no data for is named in `gaps` and produces no items; it is never filled from another client. Litware Legal, for example, has no licensing, device or backup coverage data, so its roadmap holds health items only and lists three gaps.

### AI-use policy draft (`build_ai_policy`)

Fills `governance/templates/client-ai-policy-starter.md` from:

- the MSP's `governance/AI_POLICY.md`, read at build time: the five rules (title and first sentence) and the data-class meanings. A policy that no longer has exactly five rules or seven classes refuses the run. The SHA-256 of the policy and of the template are written into the draft;
- the client's industry (`data/clients.json`, one of the `INDUSTRY_FLOOR` keys of `core/guard/classify.py`): the unknown-text floor (dental DC-4, others DC-5), an industry note, and which of DC-4 and DC-5 is this client's class;
- permitted data classes per `governance/DLP_MAPPING.md`: for each of DC-0 to DC-6, the local-model rule and the hosted or outside-tool rule. DC-6 is never. DC-4 and DC-5 never go to a hosted tool under this starter, whatever a register entry lists, because the gateway refuses them there;
- approved tools: the MSP local model gateway when `config/models.json` enables it, plus a hosted provider only when the vendor register has an entry that is `approved`, names this client, and is within its review date. Another client's entry is never shown; an expired entry approves nothing. The hosted status (`not approved`, `approved`, `expired`) is stated in the draft;
- the regimes the prototype's client fixture lists for the client, marked UNVERIFIED.

The draft says `DRAFT`, `starter, review with your counsel` and `human review required`, has blank reviewer lines and says it is not in force until reviewed. It makes no legal claim and no claim that anyone meets any law or standard.

## Acceptance

- **B73-1** roadmap recommendations list hardware lifecycle, licence and security items with the finding each rests on and a budget range from the fixture price list
- **B73-2** the AI-use policy starter is filled with the client's industry (dental or financial) defaults from the governance map and labelled 'starter, review with your counsel'
- **B73-3** no recommendation without a cited finding

Tests: `tests/py/reporting/test_b73_vcio.py` (`uv run --frozen pytest -p no:cacheprovider tests/py/reporting/test_b73_vcio.py`), functions named `test_b73_<k>_...`. `Recommendation` refuses to be built without a cited finding, and `verify_roadmap` rejects one forged around that check.

## Known limits

- UNVERIFIED: the price list, labour hours, effort and risk labels and the 90-day horizon are invented stand-ins, not any real price book or risk view. Budgets are ranges, not quotes.
- Hardware class (workstation or server) is guessed from the hostname; the licensing fixture has no device type.
- The client's industry is declared in `data/clients.json` because no client record file is committed (`config/clients` does not exist yet). A person confirms it.
- The device report is built at its fixture's pinned time; the licensing and backup coverage reports use `--generated-on`.
- A hosted entry in the register does not switch a provider on: `config/models.json` must too, and the draft says which it is.
- The policy draft is a starter written for non-lawyers. Which laws and contracts apply, the review cycle, and what follows a breach of the policy are for the client, the compliance owner and counsel.
- No model is called; the roadmap wording is fixed text. Nothing is sent.
