# B-02 Runner gates: pause switch, requester verification, contact-detail masking

Status: **built** (runner, CLI and tests). Capturing the verification on the approval page is `B-03`; this item adds only a minimal, tested bridge for it.

> Independently built prototype. Not affiliated with any vendor's or real company's systems. Synthetic data only.

Inventory: `W-IDM-02`, `W-ONB-08`, `W-SEC-04`, `W-GOV-04`, `W-CMP-05`. Controls: `C-31` (kill switch), `C-30` (requester verification), `C-11` (never-log list), `C-18` (idempotency), `C-08`, `C-09` (audit evidence). Depends on `B-01` (the `verification` flag and the `Verification` shape). Governance: `IR-2`, `AIR-11`, `EU-4`. Drift items closed: `DR-02`, `DR-03`.

## Why

A help-desk caller who talks a technician into an MFA reset is the most common way attackers get into a small business (AIR-11). B-01 put the rule on the lever. This item makes the runner refuse to break it, and gives every lead a switch that stops a lever immediately, for one client or for all of them.

## Behaviour

### Pause switch (B02-1)

- `LeverRunner.pause(lever, identity, reason, client=None)` and `LeverRunner.resume(...)`. CLI: `python -m core.levers pause <lever> [--client <id>] --as <lead> --reason <text>`, `resume <lever> [--client <id>] --as <lead> [--reason <text>]`, `paused` (lists active pauses).
- Only an identity with role `lead` may pause or resume. A client pause needs a lead scoped to that client; any lead may pause or resume a lever for all clients (IR-2: the on-call lead, immediately). No approval record is involved.
- A paused lever is denied in **dry-run and execute**, for every identity, with the reason `lever paused by <id>: <reason>`. A global pause wins over a client pause. The pause check runs right after the lever lookup and mode check, before params, roles and approvals.
- Pause and resume each write one audit record: `agent` = the lead, `client` = the client or `null` for all clients, `lever`, `mode` = `pause` or `resume`, `params` = `{scope, reason}`, `result.applied` = true. A denied pause or resume is also one record. Pausing an already-paused lever is `ok`, `applied: false`, and keeps the first pause. Resuming a lever that is not paused is denied.
- The reason is one line, 1-200 characters. Unknown levers are denied.
- Storage: `pauses.json` (`{"version": 1, "pauses": [...]}`) beside the audit log, so the CLI, the queue and the page share pauses on one state folder. Writes are atomic (audit first, then the file). An unreadable or unknown-version file denies **every** call on that runner: fail closed.

### Requester verification (B02-2)

- Executing a lever with `verification: "requester"` (`user.reset-mfa`, `user.disable`) needs an approval whose `verification` is set. Without it: denied with `<lever> needs a recorded requester verification (C-30)`, and audited.
- The checks run after the approval checks (lead, scope, four-eyes, exact binding), so those keep their own reasons. Then: `verification.by` must be a known identity scoped to the client, and `verification.at` must not be more than 5 minutes ahead of the runner's clock.
- With a valid verification the execute record's `result.verification` is `{method, by, at, ref}`.
- Dry-runs (proposals) need no verification. A write lever without the flag executes without one, as before.
- CLI: `invoke ... --execute --approved-by <lead> --verified <method> [--verified-by <id>] [--verified-ref <ref>]`. The verifier defaults to the approver and the time to now. `--verified` without `--approved-by`, or a `finding-reference` without a ref, is a usage error (exit 2).
- Bridge for `B-03` (outside this item's files, kept minimal): `ProposalQueue.decide(..., verification=None)` passes the verification into the approval; the page's `POST /decide` accepts an optional `verified=<method>` field, recorded as verified by the deciding lead at the time of the post, and returns 400 for a method outside the allowed list. The page shows no field for it yet: that, and the server-side rules about the number-on-file source, are `B03-2`.

### Contact-detail masking (B02-3)

- The **audit copy** of every diff is masked (`mask_change` in `core/levers/runner.py`); the `InvokeResult.diff` returned to the caller is untouched.
- Under `/users/<id>/authentication/methods`, a method keeps only `@odata.type` and `id`; any other method field's value becomes `[MASKED]`.
- Anywhere, the keys `phoneNumber`, `mobilePhone`, `businessPhones`, `faxNumber`, `otherMails`, `emailAddress`, `streetAddress`, `postalCode` are masked, and a string holding an international phone number (`+` then digits) is masked as a backstop. Paths are kept: they hold only ids and field names.
- `ticket.get` (and every read lever) already writes no `data`; this item adds the test that proves no ticket field reaches the record (C-11 gap).

### Crash path (B02-4)

- Every error path now records the idempotency key, including `lever crashed: <Type>` (DR-03) and the "read lever tried to change state" path. Enforcement of the key (STD-LV-6a) stays out of scope.

## Acceptance

- **B02-1** `python -m core.levers pause <lever> [--client <id>] --as <lead> --reason <text>` pauses a lever globally or for one client; a paused lever is denied in dry-run and execute with reason `lever paused by <id>: <reason>`; pause and resume each write one audit record; only a `lead` may pause or resume, and pausing needs no approval (IR-2).
- **B02-2** executing a lever with `verification: "requester"` (user.reset-mfa, user.disable) without an approval whose `verification.method` is set is denied with reason `needs a recorded requester verification (C-30)` and audited; with it, the audit record carries method, by and at.
- **B02-3** after a user.reset-mfa execute on the Contoso fixture, no audit record contains a phone-shaped value or an authentication-method field other than type and id (DR-02); the dry-run result returned to the caller still shows the full diff; a `ticket.get` read leaves no ticket field in its audit record (closes the C-11 test gap).
- **B02-4** the crash path records the idempotency key (DR-03); every pre-existing Phase 1 test passes, except demo-fallback expectations that change by adding `--verified`, each listed in the PR.

Tests: `tests/py/levers/test_b02_runner_gates.py`, one or more `test_b02_<k>_...` per id. Run with `uv run --frozen pytest tests/py/levers`.

## Out of scope

- The visible verification field, allowed-method rules and number-on-file source on the approval page (`B-03`).
- Two approvals for R3 levers (`B-28`), rate caps (`C-32`), idempotency enforcement (STD-LV-6a).
- Audit record schema v2 (STD-AU-2): `pause`/`resume` use the existing `mode` field; the verifier is unchanged.
