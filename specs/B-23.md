# B-23 Duplicate, related-ticket and outage grouping

Status: **built** (workflow, CLI, `ticket.link` lever on the mock PSA state, eval, tests). Suggests only; a link is a lead-approved write. Synthetic data only.

> Independently built prototype. Not affiliated with any vendor's or real company's systems. Synthetic data only.

Inventory (impact chains): `W-SD-03` (duplicate and related ticket detection), `W-SD-08` (outage and widespread-issue detection). Controls: `C-27` (one client per unit of work), `C-41` (eval before promotion), `C-05` (approval for writes). Depends on `B-14` (registry fragments), `B-21` (Phase 2 gold set), `B-18` (PSA seam). Standards: `STD-LV`, `STD-ID`, `STD-TS`.

## Why

A dispatcher spends part of every day finding out that three people are describing one problem, and a widespread issue looks like ten unrelated tickets. Duplicates get worked twice; an outage is not seen as one event until someone notices the pattern. The mantra applies to the false positive too: a wrong link hides one person's different problem inside another ticket, so the code only suggests, only within one client, and the write is dry-run, diffed and approved.

## Behaviour

`python -m workflows.duplicates <client> [--as-of <UTC time>] [--history]` prints ids only: duplicate groups, outage groups, and tickets a person must read.

- **Pool (isolation).** Every entry point takes the client and drops every ticket of another client before anything is classified, compared or counted. Resolved tickets are dropped unless `open_only=False` (history, for evals). A ticket the injection detector flags is left out and listed.
- **Topic.** Words in `workflows/duplicates/config.json` map a ticket to one topic (and so a category). A word in the subject counts 2, in the body 1; a tie goes to the topic listed first. No topic means the ticket is never grouped.
- **Duplicates.** `candidates_for(ticket, tickets, config)`: same client's open tickets, same topic, created within the window (`duplicate`); or same category, different topic, within the window and word overlap (Jaccard) at least `related_min_similarity` (`related`). `group_client` also returns duplicate groups: two or more tickets of one topic whose creation times chain within the window.
- **Outage groups.** N or more tickets of one category whose creation times chain within the window, from at least `outage_min_requesters` different people. N (`outage_min_tickets`, 3), the window (`window_hours`, 4) and the requester minimum (2) are in the workflow's config.
- **Links.** `propose_links(group, runner)` dry-runs `ticket.link` from the group's first ticket to each other member as the agent. Nothing is executed by the workflow.
- **`ticket.link`** (`levers/registry.d/ticket.json`, `levers/python/ticket.py`): `ticket_id`, `related_id`, `link_type` (`duplicate`, `related`, `outage-group`). Class write, risk `R1`, approval lead, `dry_run_role` agent, `required_role` technician. It sets `links[<other id>] = <type>` on both tickets of the client's own state, so the diff is one `add` per ticket (or one `replace` per ticket when the type changes) and a second run changes nothing. A ticket of another client answers exactly like an absent ticket. A link never closes, merges or reassigns a ticket.
- **Notice template** `workflows/notify/templates/ticket.link.md` (every write lever needs one, B04-2).

## Acceptance

- **B23-1** Candidates come only from the same client's open tickets: tickets of other clients are dropped before classification, so a spy on `classify` and `similarity` never sees one, and no group on the gold set contains a ticket of another client. Resolved tickets are not candidates by default. Results carry ids, topics and counts, never ticket text. A ticket flagged by the injection detector is neither grouped nor offered as a candidate and is listed for a person.
- **B23-2** A `ticket.link` R1 write lever links two tickets in the PSA mock (the client's mock tenant state) in dry-run with a diff, as an agent; executing needs a technician and a lead's approval bound to the exact params, is convergent on a second run, and refuses another client's ticket in the same words as an absent one. The notice template exists.
- **B23-3** Outage grouping raises one group when N or more tickets for one client share a category and window; N and the window are workflow config and changing them changes the result. Precision and recall on the 13 gold duplicate groups (Phase 2 set) are reported, labelled fixture-based, in `evals/duplicates/report.md`; the test checks the committed report equals a fresh run.

Tests: `tests/py/duplicates/test_b23_duplicates.py` (run with `uv run --frozen pytest -p no:cacheprovider tests/py/duplicates`). Eval: `uv run --frozen python -m evals.duplicates.report`.

## Known limits

- The topic words were written with the Phase 2 tickets in view, so the pair scores are in-sample and say the pipeline works, not how well it detects duplicates in real tickets (D-022).
- One false outage alarm remains on the gold set (two scanner tickets and a label-printer ticket, one category, one afternoon); that is the cost of the category-level rule the item specifies.
- The lever works on the mock tenant file's tickets (`T-1001` style). The Phase 2 tickets live in a separate gold fixture, so a real wiring of the two is the real PSA adapter's job (Q-01).
- `ticket.link` has no `undo` lever yet (an unlink lever is a later, separate item); undo today is a technician editing the PSA.
- `workflows/notify`'s `draft_notice` needs a `upn` param and so cannot yet fill the `ticket.link` notice; the template is in place for when it takes a ticket-based recipient.
