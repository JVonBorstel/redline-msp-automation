# B-70 M365 backup coverage, retention and immutability review, DR plan upkeep

Status: **built** (workflow, CLI, fixtures, tests). Read-only: nothing changes a backup policy, a plan or a tenant. Synthetic data only. Every backup field name is UNVERIFIED.

> Independently built prototype. Not affiliated with any vendor's or real company's systems. Synthetic data only.

Inventory: `W-BDR-05` (Microsoft 365 backup coverage check), `W-BDR-08` (backup retention and immutability review), `W-BDR-06` (disaster recovery plan upkeep and tabletop exercise); all R0 reports. Controls: `C-21`, `C-39`. Depends on `B-68` (the backup adapter and its UNVERIFIED stand-in fields). Standards: `STD-DC`, `STD-TS`.

## Why

Ransomware does not find the one mailbox nobody backed up. The new hire is not added to the backup policy, a retention setting is quietly shortened, no copy is immutable, and the DR plan has not been read since the client moved its files to SharePoint. A weekly/quarterly/yearly read-only report finds these before an incident does.

## Behaviour

`python -m workflows.backup_coverage` (module `workflows/backup_coverage/report.py`):

- **`report <client> [--as-of YYYY-MM-DD] [--stale-months N] [--out DIR]`** writes `build/backup-coverage/<client>/<client>.json` and `<client>.md`, each with a `.sha256` sidecar in `sha256sum` format. The JSON is canonical (sorted keys, two-space indent, ASCII, trailing newline), names the SHA-256 of the Markdown (`markdown_sha256`) and of its source fixture (`sources`), and carries `as_of` (default: the fixture's pinned date, never the clock) and `data_class` DC-3 (it names tenant users and mailboxes). The same inputs give the same bytes.
- **Input** `workflows/backup_coverage/data/<client>.json` (Contoso Dental, Fabrikam Capital): the client's requirement (`min_retention_days`, `immutability_required`, `dr_max_age_months`), an inventory of users, mailboxes and SharePoint sites, the backup policies, and the DR plan sections with their last review date. The file must name the client it is filed under; a client with no file is refused, not reported empty. Unknown keys fail the load. The data lives under `workflows/backup_coverage/` because that is what B-70 owns; mailbox ids and addresses and site ids are the ones in `fixtures/tenant/<client>/mailboxes.json` and `fixtures/sharepoint/<client>.json`, and a test checks they agree.
- **Coverage (B70-1).**
  - In scope: users that are enabled members (a disabled account and a guest are listed as out of scope with the reason), all mailboxes, all sites.
  - A policy of service `Exchange` covers mailboxes, `OneDrive` covers users, `SharePoint` covers sites. A policy covers an object when it is `active` and its scope is `all` (minus `excluded`) or `selected` and lists the object. A `paused` policy covers nothing.
  - Each uncovered object carries a reason: only a paused policy covers it, it is excluded from a named policy, or no policy lists it.
  - A scope id that is not in the inventory is counted in `gaps.unknown_scope_ids` and covers nothing.
- **Policies (B70-1).** Per policy, issues: `retention-below-requirement` (retention below the client's minimum; equal passes), `retention-not-stated` (null), and, only when the client requires immutability, `not-immutable` and `immutability-not-stated`.
- **DR plan (B70-2).** A section is `stale` when its last review is before `as_of` minus N whole calendar months (N from the client, or `--stale-months`; a day past the end of the target month is clamped), `never-reviewed` when it has no date, else `current`. A section reviewed exactly N months ago is current. `age_days` counts from `as_of`.
- **Tabletop outline (B70-2).** A draft (`status: draft`, banner: "Draft. A person owns the plan and runs the exercise...") built from the data: scenario, objectives, participants (owners of the stale sections, else of all), injects (a standard ransomware inject plus one for each kind of uncovered object, naming the first and counting the rest, and one for the policies with findings), one discussion question per stale section, and the evidence to record. Nothing is filed, scheduled or sent.
- **Counts (B70-3).** `counts` carries objects total, in scope, covered, uncovered; policies total and with findings; DR sections total and stale. `reconciles` is true only when, per kind, total = in scope + out of scope and in scope = covered + uncovered; policies = with findings + clean; DR sections = current + stale. `verify` recomputes this from the rows.
- **`verify <file>`** re-hashes a `.json` or `.md` against its sidecar. For the JSON it also checks canonical form, the arithmetic above, and that the sibling Markdown is the one it names, so a Markdown file replaced together with its own fresh sidecar is caught. Exit 0 match, 1 mismatch, 2 bad input.
- **Isolation (C-27).** One client per report; the output names no other client or id prefix. Free text from the data (names, titles) is untrusted: text the injection detector flags is replaced by `(text withheld)` and counted in `gaps.text_withheld`; Markdown cells are single-line with `|` escaped.

## Acceptance

- **B70-1** lists users, mailboxes and sites not covered by M365 backup per client and policies without immutability or with retention below the client's requirement
- **B70-2** a DR plan checklist per client flags sections older than N months and produces a tabletop exercise outline draft
- **B70-3** counts reconcile to the fixtures

Tests: `tests/py/backup/test_b70_coverage.py` (`uv run --frozen pytest -p no:cacheprovider tests/py/backup/test_b70_coverage.py`), functions named `test_b70_<k>_...`. Expected figures are recomputed from the raw data files in the tests, not read from the module.

## Seeded problems (fixture-based figures)

- Contoso Dental: 18 objects, 16 in scope (a disabled account and a guest are out), 4 not covered: Lena Fischer's user and mailbox (new hire, excluded from the OneDrive policy and absent from the mailbox policy), the shared `info@` mailbox, and the Marketing site (its policy is paused). 2 of 4 policies have findings: the mailbox policy is not immutable, the OneDrive policy keeps 90 days against 365. 3 of 6 DR sections are stale (one never reviewed); one section sits exactly on the 12-month boundary and is current.
- Fabrikam Capital: 14 objects, 13 in scope, none uncovered. 2 of 3 policies have findings (OneDrive 1095 days against 2555; the SharePoint policy states neither retention nor immutability). 2 of 5 DR sections are stale against a 6-month limit.

## Known limits (UNVERIFIED)

- No backup product's API or documentation was read. Every field name (`policies`, `scope`, `retention_days`, `immutable`, `state`, `service`) is an invented stand-in; nothing claims how a vendor reports coverage or immutability. The mapping "OneDrive policy covers users" is this repo's simplification.
- The client's retention and immutability requirement and the DR review age are synthetic values, not read from a contract or a plan. Real contracts may differ per service or per data type; this models one number per client.
- Coverage is judged from policy scope only. No job result, restore or storage setting was read or tested: a covered object may still fail (that is B-68's daily review), and "immutable" is taken as stated.
- The inventory is a hand-made snapshot, not read from Microsoft Graph or Exchange Online. Teams, Planner, groups, public folders and archive mailboxes are not modelled.
- The DR plan is a list of sections with a review date; the report does not read plan content, so it cannot say a section is wrong, only that nobody has looked recently.
- Report-only: no audit record is written and no identity is checked (the fixtures are synthetic, and the report is R0). A real deployment would run it under the lever runner's audit chain (B-42).
- Nothing schedules the run; weekly, quarterly and yearly cadences (inventory) are a person's or the scheduler's job.
