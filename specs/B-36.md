# B-36 Offboarding change plan with completion proof

Status: **built** (Python workflow, config, synthetic LOB list, tests). Synthetic data only. Every vendor field in the fixtures is UNVERIFIED.

> Independently built prototype. Not affiliated with any vendor's or real company's systems. Synthetic data only.

Inventory (impact chains): `W-ONB-08` (block sign-in, revoke sessions), `W-ONB-09` (mailbox conversion and auto-reply), `W-ONB-10` (OneDrive transfer), `W-ONB-11` (license removal after the retention window), `W-ONB-13` (line-of-business apps), `W-ONB-14` (completion proof). Controls: `C-05` (dry-run first), `C-06` (lead approval bound to lever, client and params), `C-08`, `C-33`, `C-35`. Standards: `STD-PR`, `STD-AU`, `STD-TS`. Builds on `B-29`, `B-31`, `B-32`, `B-33`.

## Why

A leaver's access must be provably gone, which protects the client's patients and customers. Today a technician ticks a checklist by hand (`W-ONB-14`) and has to remember to come back for the license (`W-ONB-11`).

## Behaviour

- **Package** `workflows/offboarding/` (`offboarding.py`, `__main__.py`, `config.json`, `data/lob-apps.json`, `README.md`). No model is called. Plan and verify never execute a write lever.
- **Plan.** Steps in `config.json` order: block sign-in, revoke sessions, convert mailbox to shared, auto-reply, OneDrive manager grant (end date = as-of + 14 days), one group removal per group, one license removal per license. Each step is dry-run through the runner (audited, as the requesting technician) and, if the dry-run shows a change, queued as one proposal for a lead. A step with no change is `already-met`; a refused dry-run is `refused` with the reason; a step that cannot be built is `skipped` with the reason. Nothing is dropped.
- **Retention.** A license step whose `retentionEndsOn` is on or after the as-of date is `deferred`: not dry-run, not proposed. A license with no recorded window is `skipped` (the lever refuses it too). The date comes from `as_of`, never the wall clock.
- **Manual and checklist.** Device steps are listed `manual until W6` with no lever. LOB apps are a checklist from `data/lob-apps.json`, a synthetic stand-in for the client record (see Deviations).
- **Verify.** Read-only. Compares sign-in blocked, zero sessions, shared mailbox, auto-reply on, OneDrive grant with the planned end date, no group membership, license gone (or `pending-retention` inside its window). Writes a completion record: client, upn, as-of, every step with target state and what was observed, `not_met`, `pending_retention`, `complete`, manual steps, LOB checklist, the plan's audit seqs, the audit head, and a `sha256` over the body.
- **Reconcile.** Verifies the audit chain (and head pin when present), the record's own `sha256`, the pinned head, that each dry-run seq exists with the same lever, client and params, and that each step in its target state has an applied execute record.

## Acceptance

- **B36-1** The plan orders block sign-in, revoke sessions, mailbox conversion and auto-reply, OneDrive grant, group removal, and license removal after the retention window; device steps are `manual until W6` and LOB apps are a checklist from the client record.
- **B36-2** After execution a read-only verify step checks every target state and writes a completion record; any step not in its target state is listed, not hidden (`W-ONB-14`).
- **B36-3** The completion record is part of the client's evidence (it reconciles with the audit records of the plan).

Tests: `tests/py/offboarding/test_b36_offboarding.py` (every test named `test_b36_<k>_...`). Tests that run a lever skip when `pwsh` is missing.

## Deviations (for a reviewer to overrule)

- **LOB source.** No client schema, fixture or doc has a line-of-business app field, so `data/lob-apps.json` holds an invented list per client, labelled as a stand-in. Adding the field to `schemas/client.schema.json` is a separate item.
- **Queue source.** `core/queue/store.py` allows the sources `triage`, `baseline` and `rollback`. `core/` is not owned here, so proposals use `baseline` (set in `config.json`); the `ref` starts `offboard-`. A later change can add `offboarding`.
- **Completion record is not an audit entry.** The audit modes and events are closed sets in `core/audit`. The record pins the chain head and the plan's seqs instead.

## Out of scope

Executing steps (a lead approves each proposal in the queue), device wipe (W6), forwarding (not a lever, `W-M365-05`), the notice to the person (`workflows/notify` templates exist per lever and are not called here), and a job that ends the OneDrive grant on its date.
