# B-44 Alert enrichment and risky sign-in review

Status: **built** (read levers, mock fixture, workflow, CLI, tests). Reads and recommends; never changes anything. Synthetic data only.

> Independently built prototype. Not affiliated with any vendor's or real company's systems. Synthetic data only.

Inventory: `W-SEC-02`, `W-IDM-05`, `W-SD-07`. Controls: `C-20`, `C-27`, `C-12`. Depends on `B-43` (alert grouping). Standards: `STD-LV`, `STD-FX`, `STD-SC`.

## Why

The analyst opens an alert and needs the person, their devices and their recent sign-ins in one place, with where each fact came from. For accounts the sign-in risk engine flags, the analyst needs the containment steps a lead could approve, without anything running.

## Behaviour

- **`risk.list-users`** (`levers/registry.d/risk.json`, `levers/python/risk.py`): class read, `R0`, approval none, technician role. Reads `fixtures/tenant/<client>/risky-users.json`, a family file in the Graph `riskyUser` shape. Params `riskLevel` (optional) and `limit`. Highest risk first. A client with no file gets `{"value": [], "note": ...}`; the note says P2 is not confirmed, so an empty list is never read as all clear.
- **`risk.user-facts`, `risk.device-facts`** (same files): read levers for the pack. Needed because no registered lever returned user or device facts. User facts omit phone numbers and method ids. Devices come from `workflows/factpack/fixtures/devices.json` (the B-37 stand-in, picked by the client's own id); this change owns no device family file.
- **`enrich(runner, client, alert, identity)`** (`workflows/enrichment/`): calls `risk.user-facts`, `risk.device-facts` and `signins.list` only. Each fact is `{fact, lever, field, sensitive}`. Missing data is a `gaps` entry. Names and titles that the injection detector flags are replaced by a fixed sentence (C-12). Times and places are `sensitive` and left out of `prompt_text` (DC-3).
- **`review_risky_users(runner, client, identity)`**: calls `risk.list-users`; for at-risk users with high risk it recommends `user.revoke-sessions` and `user.reset-mfa`, for medium `user.revoke-sessions`. Names and params only, `executed: False`; the names are checked against the registry. A lead approves any of them through the normal write path.
- **CLI**: `python -m workflows.enrichment pack <client> <upn>` and `... review <client>`.

## Acceptance

- **B44-1** an enrichment pack adds user, device and recent sign-in facts through read levers only, each fact citing its lever and field
- **B44-2** `risk.list-users` (R0) reads the mock riskyUsers shape (Entra ID P2 dependency noted UNVERIFIED per client); the review lists recommended levers, never executes
- **B44-3** no read result enters the audit log; packs are built under one client

Tests: `tests/py/enrichment/test_b44_enrichment.py`, named `test_b44_<k>_...`.

## Known limits

- Mock only. Entra ID P2 and the `riskyUser` field names and enum values are UNVERIFIED (no Graph page was read); the fixture and every result say so.
- Only Contoso Dental has a risky-users file; Fabrikam shows the empty-with-note path.
- Device alerts get a gap, not facts: no read lever finds a device by id, and this change cannot add one outside its files.
- Device data is the B-37 stand-in list, whose field names are UNVERIFIED. No Priya device is on record, so her pack shows that gap.
- The pack takes any object with `id`, `client`, `entity_kind`, `entity_id`; wiring it to B-43 groups is not part of this change.
- The recommendation rule (high and medium only) is plain code chosen on the fixtures, not tuned.
