# B-58 KB drafts from resolved tickets; stale and conflicting article detection

Status: **built** (draft generator, detector, CLI, eval, tests). Offline: no model is called, no network. Read-only for every knowledge base: nothing publishes a draft or edits an article. Synthetic data only.

> Independently built prototype. Not affiliated with any vendor's or real company's systems. Synthetic data only.

Inventory: `W-KB-01` (KB article drafting from resolved tickets), `W-KB-05` (stale and conflicting article detection). Controls: `C-12`, `C-25`, `C-41`. Depends on `B-57` (cited retrieval; through it `B-56`). Also reads `B-59` (documentation upkeep findings).

## Why

Resolved tickets are the best source of new KB articles and the worst place to copy from blindly: they hold requester names, patient details, secrets, another client's habits and text a stranger wrote. Old articles quietly go stale or disagree with each other. This item drafts articles from what a technician recorded as the resolution, and lists articles a person should re-check, while keeping a human between the draft and the KB.

## Behaviour: drafts (`workflows/kb_drafts/drafts.py`)

`uv run --frozen python -m workflows.kb_drafts drafts <client> [--out DIR]`, or `build_drafts(client)` then `write_drafts(set, out)`. Default output `build/kb-drafts/<client>/`: per draft `DRAFT-<ticket>.md` and `.json`, plus `index.md` and `index.json` (with a hash). Same inputs give the same bytes.

Inputs, all read-only: the client's tickets `fixtures/tickets/phase2/<client>.json` (B-21 shape) and the recorded resolutions `workflows/kb_drafts/data/resolutions/<client>.json` (invented: ticket id, title, tags and the steps the technician recorded). The tickets carry no resolution text of their own, which is why the resolutions are a separate fixture.

- A ticket with `status: resolved` and a recorded resolution becomes a draft: `draft_id = DRAFT-<ticket id>`, `label: DRAFT`, `status: draft`, `sources = [<ticket id>]`.
- **Steps come only from the recorded resolution.** The ticket subject and body are screened and never copied.
- **Redaction.** Title and steps go through `core.guard.redact_prompt` together: PHI, NPI and PII become typed numbered placeholders (`[PATIENT_NAME_1]`, `[DATE_OF_BIRTH_1]`); a placeholder already present is left as written; a secret shape withholds the draft (nothing printed). Before that, the addresses and name words of every requester and affected user in the client's tickets become `[USER]`, other addresses `[EMAIL]` and technician ids `[TECHNICIAN]`.
- **One client only.** Only that client's ticket file and resolution file are read. A record that names another client, or whose ticket is not one of this client's, is listed under `rejected` by ticket id and never drafted. A resolution file whose `client` differs raises `DraftError`.
- **Injection.** Ticket subject, body, title and steps are scanned with `detect_injection`; any hit withholds the whole ticket (`withheld`, reason `injection`, rule ids only, no text).
- Not drafted: a ticket not `resolved` (`skipped: not-resolved`), a resolution with no steps (`no-recorded-resolution`).
- **Human publish step.** Every draft says DRAFT first, `human_publish.required = true`, `done = false`, and the page tells the reader a person must review, edit and publish it by hand in the KB tool. No function sets `done`, no code path writes to a KB, and there is no lever.

## Behaviour: stale and conflicting articles (`workflows/kb_drafts/detect.py`)

`uv run --frozen python -m workflows.kb_drafts detect <client> [--as-of DATE] [--max-age-days N] [--out DIR]`, or `detect_report(client, kb_dir=, reviews_dir=, as_of=, max_age_days=, drafts=)`. Defaults: the B-56 corpus `fixtures/kb/`, the B-59 documentation fixtures `workflows/doc_upkeep/data/` (review dates), N = 365, `as_of` pinned by the review fixture (the clock is never read). Writes `<out>/<client>.json` and `.md`, default `build/kb-detect/`.

- **Stale**: an article last reviewed more than N days before `as_of`, or one the B-59 report lists as stale for a reason other than its own age limit (it documents a device, contact or licence observed state no longer shows). The B-59 finding ids are listed.
- **Conflicting**: for each article of the client, each sentence is a B-57 `search` query (client namespace plus `msp-internal`, filter applied in the store, fail-closed on a leaky store); a sentence of another article of the **same client** in the results that contradicts it under a lexical rule is a conflict, listed with **both article ids**, both sentences (redacted) and the retrieval score. Rules (`text.py`): `opposite-instruction` (nearly the same words, one negated), `different-value` (same words, different numbers), `opposite-action` (enable/disable, allow/block). Passages from `msp-internal` are counted, never compared.
- **Drafts** passed in are checked the same way against the existing articles (`draft_conflicts`).
- Safety: an article with instruction-like text is listed in `flagged_articles` and neither compared nor quoted; vault entries are counted and never read; quoted sentences pass the redaction gate.

## The eval (`evals/kb_drafts/`)

`uv run --frozen python -m evals.kb_drafts.run` rewrites `report.md` (a test checks it equals a fresh run). It reports **step coverage** (gold step fragments, `gold.json`, found in the drafts of tickets that should produce one), the outcome per ticket (drafted, withheld, skipped, rejected), detection against the planted KB (`workflows/kb_drafts/data/kb/`, `data/reviews/`: missed and spurious), and a leak sweep over every written file for planted canaries, other clients' articles, requester names and PHI. All fixture-based.

## Acceptance

- **B58-1** a resolved ticket produces an article draft with steps and the ticket id as source; PHI placeholders stay placeholders
- **B58-2** articles older than N days or contradicting another article on the same topic (same client) are listed with both ids
- **B58-3** drafts are never published by code; eval reports step coverage fixture-based

Tests: `tests/py/kb/test_b58_kb_drafts.py` (`uv run --frozen pytest -p no:cacheprovider tests/py/kb`), functions named `test_b58_<k>_...`, written first and committed red.

## Interpretation notes

- **"Steps and the ticket id as source"** is read as: the draft's only source is the ticket id and its steps are the recorded resolution, not text the model or code composed.
- **"Never published by code"** is checked three ways: the drafts and reports are written only under the chosen output folder (a test hashes the KB, fixtures and tickets before and after), the package imports no ingestion or store-writing name from `rag` and no network or model module (AST test), and no public name contains `publish`.
- **Lever.** Not added: the roadmap's `owns` does not include the registry, and a draft generator that only writes files for a person needs none.
- `workflows/kb_drafts` imports `detect_injection`, so `tests/py/evals/test_b79_injection_v2.py` lists it under `GUARDED_NOT_EXERCISED` (the only edit outside `owns`; its corpus is not run through this workflow).

## Known limits

- UNVERIFIED: no real ticketing export, KB tool or language model was used. The tickets are B-21's synthetic ones; the recorded resolutions are invented to plant cases. How a real PSA stores a resolution is UNKNOWN.
- Retrieval is the `HashEmbedder` word-overlap stand-in. The conflict rules are lexical: they find "restart X" against "do not restart X", "every 7 days" against "every 30 days" and enable/disable swaps. They miss paraphrase and any contradiction that needs meaning, and they can fire on two sentences about different things that share most words. The planted KB was written to be found; real recall and precision are unknown. `RELATED_MIN` (0.15) and the 0.8 overlap are prototype choices.
- Step coverage is near-trivial by construction (steps are copied); it checks that redaction and the rules do not drop steps, not that a draft is a good article. Whether the recorded steps are correct or complete is not judged.
- Redaction is the B-55 shape set. A name or fact with no recognizable shape, and in a step rather than in a form field, is not caught; the draft is a draft because a person must read it.
- Name scrubbing uses the requesters named in the client's tickets, so a name that is not in a ticket form field passes (and a short name word can also match an ordinary word).
- A client article is not checked against `msp-internal` runbooks, and one client's article is never compared with another's, by design; a conflict across those lines is not reported.
- A flagged article is skipped whole, not sentence by sentence; one poisoned sentence hides the article's clean ones from the comparison.
- B-59 review dates are a fixture; the KB manifests carry none. Stale detection depends on that fixture being kept up.
- Nothing runs on a schedule, files a ticket or edits the KB; running the commands is manual.
