# B-94 AI incident response runbook, notification templates review and tabletop

Status: **built** (runbook, three templates, one recorded tabletop, tests). Documents for people; nothing is sent by software. Synthetic data only. Not legal advice.

> Independently built prototype. Not affiliated with any vendor's or real company's systems. Synthetic data only.

Inventory (impact chain): `W-GOV-04`. Controls: `C-34` (incident runbook and review), `C-35` (notification templates), `C-31` (pause switch), `C-33` (rollback). Depends on: B-41 (rollback), B-02 (pause). Also relies on B-42 (per-client audit chains) and B-55 (data classes). Standards: `STD-DC`.

## Why

When something goes wrong, the person is restored first and the client hears from a human. The steps `IR-1` to `IR-9` already exist as a table in `docs/map/05-governance.md` section 7; this item turns them into commands that exist in the repo, three plain-language drafts a person sends, and one rehearsal.

## Behaviour

- `docs/runbooks/ai-incident-response.md`: one section per step `IR-1` to `IR-9`, with the same ids and names, in order, as the governance map. Pause (`core.levers pause`), scope (an audit filter over `audit/*.jsonl`, `core.levers verify`, `core.levers export`), rollback (`workflows.rollback`) and re-enable (`core.levers resume`) are PowerShell code blocks over placeholders (`<lever>`, `<client>`, ...) that a table defines.
- `governance/templates/notifications/`: `affected-person.md`, `client-contact.md`, `privacy-officer.md` (`string.Template`, `$name` placeholders), and a README that documents the placeholders and reviews the per-lever drafts of `workflows/notify/templates/` for incident use. Each template opens with "Draft. A person reviews and sends this." and holds a `Timelines` list.
- `docs/TABLETOP_2026.md`: the wrong user disabled at Contoso Dental, on synthetic data only: scenario, inject script, a timeline with times, what worked, lessons with follow-ups.
- No legal deadline is invented. The only deadlines quoted are BA-5 (60 days) and GLBA-7 (30 days, 500 or more consumers) as `docs/map/05-governance.md` states them; every deadline line, those two included, says "confirm with counsel". State law and contract terms are named, not quoted.

## Acceptance

- **B94-1** The runbook follows `IR-1` to `IR-9` with commands for pause, scope (audit query) and rollback that exist in the repo. Checked by: heading order against the governance map; every command's module, subcommand, flags and cited paths exist; every placeholder is defined; the commands run end to end on a synthetic wrong disable.
- **B94-2** Notification templates for the affected person, client contact and privacy officer are plain language and list the timelines as "confirm with counsel". Checked by: grade at most 8, no sentence over 25 words, no internal words, word caps, every deadline line says "confirm with counsel", no phone number, address or name in the template body; the same checks run on deliberately spoiled copies and must object.
- **B94-3** One tabletop on synthetic tenants (wrong user disabled at Contoso Dental) is recorded with times and lessons. Checked by: the nine steps in order with times, lessons each with a follow-up, the ticket text and ids match the fixtures, lesson L-1 still matches the notify code.

Tests: `tests/py/governance/test_b94_incident_response.py` (run with `uv run --frozen pytest -p no:cacheprovider tests/py/governance`). The end-to-end test needs PowerShell 7.4+ (`pwsh`) and skips when it is missing.
