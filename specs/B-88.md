# B-88 Teams approval card, Outlook draft and SharePoint ingestion mocks; site creation lever

Status: **mocked** (builders, mock adapters, the `site.*` levers on the mock tenant, the dry-run `workflows/teams_approvals/`, tests). All three acceptance parts are built as mocks. No Microsoft call is made, no credential is held, nothing is posted or delivered, no real site is created. Synthetic data only. **Every Microsoft payload shape here is UNVERIFIED** (see Known limits).

> Independently built prototype. Not affiliated with any vendor's or real company's systems. Synthetic data only.

Inventory: `W-TOOL-10`, `W-RPT-03`, `W-M365-06`. Controls: `C-06`, `C-19`, `C-26`. Depends on `B-03` (approval page and queue) and `B-56` (RAG ingestion). Standards: `STD-LV`, `STD-SC`, `STD-FX`.

## Scope of this build

Built, second pass: the `site.new` lever with its read-back `site.get` and its registered undo `site.remove-empty` (B88-3: `levers/registry.d/site.json`, `levers/python/site.py`, `workflows/notify/templates/site.new.md` and `site.remove-empty.md`), the `site` lever namespace approval record `AC-032` in `governance/model_tool_approvals.json`, `site` in the injection tripwire's lever namespaces (`core/guard/detect.py`), and `workflows/teams_approvals/` (classified in `tests/py/evals/test_b79_injection_v2.py` as a workflow that does not import the guard).

## Behaviour

Package `adapters/m365/` (standard library plus this repo's `core`, `adapters` and `rag`; a test checks there is no network, mail or Microsoft library and no secret-shaped text).

### Teams approval card (`teams_card.py`)

- `build_approval_card(queue, proposal_id, base_url=...)` returns a Teams message payload (an Adaptive Card attachment) for one **pending** proposal. Unknown, malformed or already decided ids raise `CardError`. It reads the queue and the proposal's own client audit chain; it writes nothing.
- The card shows: lever and client, proposal id, the person affected (`upn`), requester, source and ref, the lever's **L1 impact line**, the **dry-run diff as the audit holds it** (masked; "No dry-run on record ... Do not approve it." when there is none), the **undo lever** or `no undo lever: manual undo`, and the requester's reason.
- **A card button can never approve.** The card has exactly one action, `Action.OpenUrl` to `http://127.0.0.1:<port>/#<proposal id>`. No `Action.Submit`, `Action.Execute`, input, token or CSRF value. The link base must be exactly plain `http` on `127.0.0.1` or `localhost` (optional port, nothing else); anything else raises `CardError`.
- `handle_card_response(queue, response, base_url=...)` is the rule for a card response that arrives anyway (forged, or a future card version). It **never decides**: `CardResponseResult.decided` is always `False`, `ProposalQueue.decide` is never called, the claimed identity, verification fields and any `client` in the response are ignored. If the response names a pending proposal, a `denied` record is audited in that proposal's own client chain by the fixed identity `teams-card` (never under the claimed person's name, since nothing proves who sent it) and the result points to the approval page. Otherwise nothing is written.
- Untrusted text (reason, UPN, diff values) is made one plain line: control and invisible characters removed, `[` `]` become parentheses and `<` `>` are removed (no link or tag can form), long values are cut. Text the injection tripwire flags is replaced by a withheld notice (C-12). The card must stay under 20 000 bytes.
- **Interpretation of B88-1, "a card response is validated exactly like a UI decision (scope, four-eyes, verification)".** Read as: a card response gets none of the UI's decision power. Scope, four-eyes and requester verification are enforced where a decision is made, on the approval page behind a signed-in lead (B-03, B-89); a card cannot carry a sign-in, so its response is audited as a refusal and sent to the page. If the owner wants a card click to decide, it needs a verified Entra sign-in on the card path first (not built).

### Outlook draft (`outlook.py`)

- `OutlookDraftMock(client, binding, allowed_domains=...)` is bound to one client (the one-client rule of `adapters/base.py`). `create_digest_draft(week_of, mailbox, to, items, dry_run=True)`, `list_drafts()`, `get_draft(id)`. **There is no method that delivers a message**, and a test reads the module so none can be added unnoticed.
- **Dry run is the default**: the payload is returned and nothing is stored. With `dry_run=False` the draft is stored in the mock; creating the same content again gives the same draft (id derived from content).
- Message shape: `subject`, `body` (`contentType` always `text`), `toRecipients`, `from`, `isDraft: true`. The subject is built from the client id and the week only. The body starts with a "DRAFT - not delivered" banner and lists the digest lines.
- Recipients: 1 to 10 plain addresses, each on one of the client's own domains passed by the caller; no allowed domains means none allowed. A display name, several addresses in one string, a line break or an outside domain refuses the draft.
- Digest lines: a line holding a DC-6 secret-shaped value refuses the whole draft (never echoed); a line that reads like an instruction (including with hidden characters) is withheld; other personal data becomes B-55 placeholders; lines are one plain line, 300 characters at most; 1 to 50 lines.
- The "weekly digest" itself is not built (no digest generator exists in the repo); the builder takes the lines.

### SharePoint ingestion source (`sharepoint.py`)

- `SharePointMock(client, binding, root)` reads `<root>/<client>.json` once (default `adapters/m365/fixtures/`). The file's `client` must be the adapter's and its `tenantId` the binding's `external_id`, else `AdapterError`. `SharePointMock.for_fixture(client)` builds from the fixture's own tenant id.
- `sources()` returns B-56 `Source` objects (no client field). Items it cannot take are skipped with a reason code and counted in `skipped` as `(library/item position, reason)`: `malformed`, `unsafe-id`, `duplicate-id`, `unsupported-type` (only `text/plain` and `text/markdown`), `bad-name`, `no-text`, `empty`, `too-large` (over 200 000 characters).
- Site, library and path names go into the source's `tags` and `origin`, so B-56's vault rule refuses a library named like a credential store before the text is read.
- `ingest_sharepoint(adapter, job, store, embedder)` calls `rag.ingest`. **The job's client must be the adapter's**, otherwise `IngestError` before anything is read or written (an MSP job is refused too). The namespace is the job's, never anything in an item. Vault sources, DC-6 shapes (refused), personal data (redacted) and injection-like chunks (stored withheld, with the flag) follow B-56 unchanged.

### Fixtures

`adapters/m365/fixtures/contoso-dental.json` and `fabrikam-capital.json`: invented libraries, one PDF item (skipped) and one library named `Credentials` (refused) for Contoso Dental. Nothing is captured from a real tenant.

### Site levers (`levers/registry.d/site.json`, `levers/python/site.py`), B88-3

- **`site.new`** (write, **R2**, `approval: lead`, dry-run by `agent`, execute by `technician`, `client_scope: any`, the R2 default blast-radius caps of B-42). Params, all validated by the registry schema: `display_name` (1-60 letters, digits, spaces, `&`, `'`, `-`), `slug` (3-40 lowercase letters, digits, single hyphens; reserved paths such as `admin`, `sites`, `teams` refused), `upn` (the owner), optional `co_owner_upn`, `template` (`teams-team`, `team-site`, `communication-site`), optional `external_sharing` (`disabled`, the default, or `existing-guests`; anyone links and new guests are not offered). The owner is `upn` so the card shows them as the person affected and B-04 can address the notice to them.
- The handler adds one record under `sites` in that client's base state: `id` (the slug), `displayName`, `webUrl` (`https://<tenant>.sharepoint.example/sites/<slug>`, the tenant label from the owner's domain), `template`, `webTemplate`, `groupConnected`, `teamsEnabled`, `visibility: Private` (always), `sharingCapability` (`Disabled` unless existing guests were asked for), `owners`, `contentItems: 0`, `createdBy: site.new`. **The dry-run diff and the returned `site` are exactly that record.**
- **Owners must be enabled member users of that client's own mock tenant.** The handler sees only that client's state, so an owner from another client, an unknown address, a disabled account, a guest, or the same person twice is refused (`error`, nothing written). The runner's scope check stops a technician of one client from acting for another.
- Idempotent: the same params twice change nothing; the same slug with other values, or a second site with the same display name, is refused.
- **Structured request only.** `parse_site_request(request)` turns form fields (`display_name`, `slug`, `owners` as a list of one or two UPNs, `template`, `external_sharing`, `notes`) into params. Any other field refuses the request. `notes` is the only free text and is **never read for a parameter**: notes that hold an address, a URL, `/sites/` or `/teams/`, or a `slug:`/`owner=`/`url:`/`template:`/`sharing:` setting are refused, and so are notes the injection tripwire flags. A slug or owner written in free text therefore never becomes a parameter (fooled-input tests).
- **Verification after apply.** `site.get` (read, R0, technician) reads one site back by slug; `verify_applied(read_back, params)` lists every difference from the request (missing site, name, template, visibility, sharing, owners, URL). Empty means the site is as asked. The runner has no post-apply hook, so this is a separate call; the approval page (`core/queue`) does not run it yet (Known limits).
- **Undo: `site.remove-empty`** (write, R2, lead), registered as the undo of `site.new` (and `site.new` as its undo). It takes the same params and removes the site only when the record on file is exactly what `site.new` writes for them, was created by `site.new` and holds no content; it is the exact inverse, so `workflows.rollback` proposes it from the audit record (B-41). Decision: not `site.archive`, because an archive leaves the site in place and cannot restore the before-state (and Microsoft 365 Archive is a separate paid feature, UNVERIFIED); not "irreversible, manual steps", because an empty new site can be cleanly removed and an irreversible lever would have to be R3. A site with content, or one made outside `site.new`, is never removed by a lever: that stays a manual review.
- Notices: `workflows/notify/templates/site.new.md` and `site.remove-empty.md` (B-04 drafts, addressed to the owner; never sent).
- Governance: lever namespace `site` is approved as `AC-032` (R2, local, contract-test evidence `test_b88_3_site_new_dry_run_shows_the_exact_site_and_changes_nothing`); `python -m workflows.assurance.approvals check` passes. `graph_scopes` (`Sites.Create.All`, `Group.Create`, `Sites.Read.All`, `Sites.FullControl.All`) are review input only and UNVERIFIED.

### Teams approval prompts (`workflows/teams_approvals/`)

- `plan_posts(queue, client, base_url=..., dry_run=True)` builds, for each **pending** proposal of that one client in queue order, the card from `adapters.m365.build_approval_card`. A proposal that gets no card is listed in `skipped` with the adapter's reason. `dry_run=False` raises `TeamsApprovalsError` (there is no posting code); a bad client id or a link base that is not the loopback page is refused before anything is read.
- `record_dry_run(run, state_dir)` writes `<state>/teams-approvals/<client>.json`: `dry_run: true`, `posted: false`, a note, and per card the proposal id, lever, the card and its SHA-256. The same queue and audit give the same bytes.
- It never decides or writes the queue or audit: no call to `decide`, `refuse`, `propose` or `invoke` (a test reads the code), and the queue and audit bytes are unchanged after a run. No network, Teams or Graph import (a test reads the imports).
- CLI: `uv run --frozen python -m workflows.teams_approvals <client> [--state .tmp/demo] [--base-url http://127.0.0.1:8765]`; exit 0 recorded, 2 refused. README: `workflows/teams_approvals/README.md`.

## Known limits

- **UNVERIFIED against Microsoft documentation.** Written from memory, no Microsoft page opened, nothing posted or rendered: the Teams message envelope and `application/vnd.microsoft.card.adaptive` attachment; the Adaptive Card `$schema` URL, version `1.4`, `TextBlock`, `FactSet` and `Action.OpenUrl` fields; how Teams renders markdown inside `TextBlock` (the markup neutralising is a precaution, not a measured fix); the ~28 KB message limit (the card is held to 20 000 bytes); whether Teams or a Workflow connector accepts a `localhost` link at all (a loopback link opens only on the lead's own machine); the Outlook/Graph message shape (`isDraft`, `toRecipients`, `body.contentType`); the SharePoint/Graph `driveItem` shape. The draft ids are mock ids, not Graph ids.
- Nothing is posted to Teams and no draft reaches Outlook: there is no transport. A real Teams webhook, a Graph mail draft call and a Graph file download are adapters not built (the real implementation returns `real adapter not configured`).
- The card link opens the approval page's root; the page has no per-proposal anchor, so `#<id>` is a hint only.
- The card's diff is read from the proposal's dry-run audit record; if the dry-run record is missing the card says so. It reflects the dry-run, not the tenant's state at approval time.
- A card response carries no verified identity, so it is refused even from a real lead; leads still approve on the page. The refusal audit record names `teams-card`, not a person.
- SharePoint: text-like files only; no paging, no delta sync, no deletion handling (a deleted item stays in the index until re-ingested without it), no sensitivity-label or sharing-link check, and the fixture holds the extracted `text`. Vault detection is by name words only (B-56 limit); a credentials file in an ordinary library with no secret shape would be ingested.
- The Outlook builder's digest input is plain lines; the PHI/PII redaction is B-55's heuristics for synthetic text.
- Site levers: mock only, no Graph or SharePoint call; every field name, `webTemplate` id, `sharingCapability` value, the URL shape and the `graph_scopes` are UNVERIFIED. The URL host is `.sharepoint.example` on purpose. `teams-team` records `teamsEnabled: true` only; no channel, tab or membership beyond the owners is created. No members are added, no sensitivity label or retention is set, no storage quota is set. The reserved-slug list is a fixture-based choice.
- The post-apply check (`site.get` + `verify_applied`) is not run by the approval page's execute path (`core/queue`, not changed here); a technician or a later item must call it.
- `site.remove-empty` drops the mock record; a real tenant keeps a deleted site in its recycle bin for a period (from memory, UNVERIFIED). Content is counted by `contentItems` in the mock only.
- `workflows/teams_approvals/` records only; it has no history across runs and no de-duplication, and posting needs a reviewed Teams adapter, its own approval record and a secret store (none built).
