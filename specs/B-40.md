# B-40: TenantBaseline identity checks: SSPR coverage and break-glass sign-in watch

Status: built 2026-10-22. Inventory: W-IDM-13, W-IDM-08. Controls: C-21, C-20. Standards: STD-PS, STD-FX, STD-TS.

Two new read-only checks in `levers/powershell/TenantBaseline`, behind `-IncludeIdentityChecks` (default off).

- SSPR (medium). Per client: of the enabled member accounts, how many have `isSsprRegistered=true` in
  `userRegistrationDetails`. A finding when coverage is below the minimum (default 90 percent, optional config key
  `ssprMinimumCoveragePercent`). Break-glass candidates are left out of the denominator (cloud-only emergency accounts, kept
  out of self-service). A missing registration record counts as not registered, as in the MFA check. Fix is manual.
- BGSIGNIN (high). Any sign-in by a break-glass candidate (name prefix or display-name pattern from config) inside the window
  before the as-of date (default 14 days, optional config key `breakGlass.signInWindowDays`). Uses the latest of the three
  `signInActivity` timestamps, so a failed attempt counts too. Fix is manual.
- Both defaults live in the module, not in `TenantBaseline.config.json`; the config can override them.
- Summary: two extra sentences when the checks run. `Test-SummaryReconciles.ps1` has fact phrases for them, so every new
  number must match findings.json (SB-2).
- Fixture: `contoso/users.json` bg-alpha now shows a sign-in on 2026-09-27 (3 days before the pinned as-of 2026-10-01). Other
  break-glass accounts stay outside the window: bg-beta 47 days, emergency.admin 29 days, Fabrikam bg-01/02 21 days.

Seeded result (Contoso): SSPR 9 of 13 (69%) -> 1 finding; BGSIGNIN bg-alpha -> 1 finding; 16 findings with the checks on.
Fabrikam (clean): 6 of 6, no sign-in in window -> 0.
