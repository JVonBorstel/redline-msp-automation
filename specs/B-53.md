# B-53 Oversharing, Copilot readiness and labels/DLP assessment

Status: **built** (workflow, CLI, fixtures, tests). Report only. Synthetic data only.

> Independently built prototype. Not affiliated with any vendor's or real company's systems. Synthetic data only.

Inventory (impact chains): `W-M365-07`, `W-PRJ-04`, `W-M365-08`. Controls: `C-25`, `C-21`, `C-20`. Depends on: nothing. Standards: `STD-FX`, `STD-DC`, `STD-TS`.

## Why

Copilot does not surface the wrong patient file to the wrong employee. Before rollout the MSP needs to see, per client, which sites are open to everyone, shared outside, or hold sensitive content with no label, and what that costs in a score a person can check.

## Behaviour

`python -m workflows.copilot_readiness <client> [--as-of <date>] [--markdown]` reads `fixtures/sharepoint/<client>.json` and prints the report.

- **Findings.** `everyone-link` (a link with scope `anyone` or `everyone-except-external`), `external-share` (a share to a recipient of type `guest`), `unlabelled-sensitive-site` (sensitivity health, finance or legal and no label). Each has an id, the site, a severity, its points and a detail.
- **Score.** `max(0, 100 - sum(points of every finding))`, points = the kind's weight (everyone-link 15, external-share 10, unlabelled-sensitive-site 20). Weights are invented for a fixture pipeline check.
- **Recommendations.** For each sensitive category on the client's sites that no label covers a `label-gap`, and none that no DLP policy covers a `dlp-gap`. Weight 0, `action: recommendation-only`. Nothing proposes a policy change.
- **Printed with the report.** The formula, the weights, the counts and points per kind, and the fixture totals (sites, links, shares).
- **Empty and bad states.** No sites: score 100 and the note "no sites in fixture". A bad client id, an unknown client, a malformed or mismatched file, an unknown field value or a secret-like field: exit 2, nothing scored.
- **Isolation (C-20).** The client is explicit; only that client's file is opened.

## Acceptance

- **B53-1** From SharePoint/OneDrive sharing fixtures (Everyone links, external shares, unlabelled sensitive sites) the assessment scores readiness per client with every point traced to a finding.
- **B53-2** Label and DLP gaps are reported as recommendations; no policy change is proposed as a lever.
- **B53-3** The score's formula is printed with the report; counts reconcile to the fixture.

Tests: `tests/py/copilot/test_b53_copilot.py` (run with `uv run --frozen pytest -p no:cacheprovider tests/py/copilot`).

## Known limits

- Every vendor field name is UNVERIFIED: written from memory, no Microsoft page opened (`fixtures/sharepoint/SOURCES.md`).
- Weights are not calibrated on real tenants; a pass shows the pipeline works, not that real tenants look like this.
- Organization-wide links, inheritance and group membership are not modelled.
